# The project page: the task card

The card of a task: editing, subtasks, the journal, the description editor, drafts, attachments, adding tasks.

- The task card (`web/src/pages/TaskCard.tsx`, loaded lazily together with `react-markdown`): a task title in the backlog, in the session panel's head and of a locked blocker in the chain opens it; a press anywhere else on a chain row (all but its links and the meters on the right) opens the session panel, the key's link stretched over the row. An open card is the `?task=<id>` parameter in the address, so "back" on a phone closes it (the project's packages are `?pkg=1`, `&add=<plugin>` with the add dialog over them, `ProjectPackages.tsx`, `docs/packages.md`); links to other tasks inside the card replace the parameter without piling up history. Every open reads the card from the tracker; the last card read for each task stays in the tab's memory (`cardMemory.ts`, BTN-655, keyed by the project's GUID and the task, up to 200 cards, never in `localStorage`), so a card opened again shows at once without the loader while it is re-read, the answer replacing it quietly; a failed re-read shows its error above the remembered card, a "no such task" forgets it. A change made in the card reads it anew and so updates the remembered one. The card is 1040 px wide and stands 32 px from the top, not in the middle (BTN-801): while a task is read it is at its full height (`max-height`, the loader filling the body), and once the content shows, a shorter one glides the bottom edge up to it (`useHeightGlide`, keyed by the shown card's id, `--motion-appear`), so a long task never moves and the head stays put; a linked task glides back to the full height for its read, a remembered one straight between the two heights; with reduced motion and on a full-screen card below 760 px nothing glides. Its styles are `card.css` (dialog, head, body layout), `cardMain.css` (the left column), `cardSide.css` (the panel) and `cardNarrow.css` (reduced motion and narrow screens, imported last). Markdown links leading inside the tracker are text, and those with a task number open its card. HTML inside Markdown is rendered through `rehype-raw` and cleaned by `rehype-sanitize`: Huly writes its tables as HTML. The layout is two columns — the description and comments on the left, a panel on the right (on top below 900 px). The panel (BTN-1097, BTN-1283) sticks to the top of the body and is no taller than the body's visible part, so a long list of relations scrolls inside it while the description scrolls on its own; below 900 px it scrolls with the card. Under the bare action every group is a block of its own, 12 px apart — the two facts, priority with the milestone, "Runs on", the parent, the labels with their roles, the attachments, and each kind of relation (`.task-card__block`: a step lighter than the card, 1 px `--border`, `--radius`, 12 px inside); the parent and the relations carry a 3 px stripe at the left — the parent `--lamp-fix`, "Blocked by" `--lamp-waiting`, "Blocks" `--lamp-working`, "Related" `--lamp-idle`. Every label is one section label (11 px uppercase, muted, 0.04em): priority and milestone have it at the left, the other blocks (the parent too) above. The fake `?card=links` gives a card a parent and twelve related tasks. The action is "Into the chain" or "To the backlog" in a `ButtonWithTip`, what it does behind its "?", a refusal as a warn line under it. The properties start with two facts, "Estimate" (`card.estimate`) and "Time" — the agent's active time over the whole task, the `/stats` "Time" column, sent with the card as `activeS` (`docs/stats.md`); the time takes the warning tone over ×1.5 of the estimate (`cardTime.ts`, the "Within ×1.5" bound of `/stats`), a missing one is "—" and neither hides the row. Relation keys share one column width, the longest key of the card in `ch` (`--ref-key`), so the titles start on one line; where the tracker has `milestones`/`moveMilestone` (`docs/trackers.md`) the milestone is a `Select` that moves the task at once, without a confirmation: the new value shows right away and goes back with the error if the tracker refuses; a question or excluding label is a callout above the description. Under the milestone, "Runs on" (`TaskRunPick.tsx`, BTN-508, BTN-983) reads the task's run from `state.runs` (`runPickOf` in `runPick.ts`), not its labels: a provider `Select` — first the project's provider by its name with the "as the project" icon (`InheritIcon` turned down, the words in its tip, BTN-1375) — for a provider a free model field suggesting the models of the project's account for it (`SuggestField`, `useModelCatalog`), the project's model as its placeholder, and an effort `Select` under its own "Effort" label, the bare level or "default", with the levels of the provider picked, or of the project's one under "as the project" (`effortsOf`), absent where that provider takes none; a new provider clears the model and an effort it does not take (`withProvider`); the three are fields of one look, height and left edge. The card's section heads carry their number as a `task-card__count` pill, as Attachments: Comments or Journal, Subtasks with closed/total and "Closed: n of m" in its tip. It does not save on a pick: Save (or Enter in the model field), shown only when the pick differs from the run at the right of the "Runs on" label so nothing below moves, sends `POST /api/task/run` with the whole run (`runPickChange`; every field empty for no run), which touches no tracker, so the pick works on any adapter. A live session and a pending change get a note: the run takes effect from the task's next session (a record, not a touch of the session). The task's labels are plain tags, a `<Provider> | <model>` one among them. Attachments are tiles in that panel between the facts and the relations (`Attachments.tsx`): only photos and videos open, in `MediaViewer.tsx` over the whole window (arrows, ←/→, swipe, a click on a picture zooms, Esc goes back to the card), any other file downloads. The name, the pill and "Open" are a dock held to the window's bottom (BTN-1713, above the phone's safe area), the file fitted into the stage over it and centred there, a zoomed picture scrolling inside the stage, so the dock stands in one place for any file. Its top line is the name (BTN-1706) in a plaque of the pill's tone, cut in the middle so the extension stays (the full name in its tip); under it a centred pill of ‹, the counter and ›, kept at the width of "999 / 999" so the arrows never move, an arrow off rather than gone at either end, with the "Open" button beside it in the grid's right column; a single file has no pill, "Open" then centred. Below 760 px the arrows stay round at the window's sides, the dock keeps its two lines: the name, then the counter in a plaque with "Open" as an icon beside it. A picture tile is the full file loaded lazily; a video tile has no preview on purpose — without `Range` support even `preload=metadata` could pull the whole video.
- Editing in the card (BTN-771, a task of the built-in tracker, whose card carries `edit`; `CardFields.tsx`, `CardLinks.tsx`, `CardTree.tsx`, `useCardEdit.ts`, the pure rules in `cardEdit.ts`, styles in `cardEdit.css`): the card is where such a task changes, so it has no "Open in …" link and no "Into the chain"/"To the backlog" buttons — the status pick in the head does the same. The title edits in place: Enter, leaving the field, closing the card or hiding the tab saves it, Escape drops it, an empty one is not saved. What was sent stands in the field from the moment it is left until the card is read anew, and a saved description in its view the same way (BTN-1111, `useHeld.ts`); only a refusal brings the old value back. A typed edit is kept in the browser until it lands (BTN-1027, see "Drafts" below). The status is a `Select` of the seven fixed statuses: Backlog → a `ToDo` status is `task/ready`, a `ToDo`/`Active` one → Backlog `task/unready` (off with its reason while a session is live or the chain refuses the task), anything else an `edit`. Priority is a `Select`; the milestone a `SuggestField` creating a new one by its typed name, with a warning under it when the project's "Project tasks" rules leave that milestone out; labels a `SuggestField` of the project's labels and the built-in ones with their meaning, a new name creating the label. "Component" under "Milestone" (BTN-1288, `ComponentField`) is a `SuggestField` of the project's components: typing filters them, a typed name no component has is offered as "create" (sent with `create: true`), × "No component" clears it. The "Estimate" tile has a pencil at its right, muted until the tile is pointed at (BTN-1288, `CardEstimate.tsx`, `cardEstimate.css`); the whole tile is its button, so an editable card shows the estimate and time tiles even before either is known. It opens a field under the tile with "min", a "?" of the formats ("20", "1h 20m", "1.5h"; empty — no estimate) and a check button: Enter saves through `builtin/estimate` (`estimateMinutes` in `cardTime.ts`), Escape or a press outside closes it, a text it cannot read keeps it open with a red line under it. "Time" is the sessions' and never edited. Relations: "+ Add a link" opens the kind (`blocks`, `blocked by`, `relates to`, focused first) and a task search over every built-in project (`builtin/search`); a refusal stands under the search, × on a row removes the link. A subtask shows the path of its ancestors over the title (each opens its card) and a Parent field with ×; "Change the parent" (or "Pick a parent") searches the task's own project. Both searches send `for: {task, field}` (BTN-1589), and the service leaves out what it would refuse for that field: for a link the task itself and the tasks it already links by that kind, for a parent the task, its parent and its subtasks at any depth; a loop of blockers through other tasks is still found only by the refusal. A parent's "Subtasks" list (shown on every tracker's card, see the next item) gets "+ Add subtask" here, on an open task even without subtasks yet (none on a closed task): the subtask takes the parent's milestone and Todo; the first one of a task in work asks first, since the task leaves the chain while its session goes on. A parent's milestone change asks whether its open subtasks move too (yes by default). "Delete" in the card's ⋯ menu asks, listing the subtasks that become top-level; it is off while the task is current or has a live session. Every field edit sends what the field held and the new value; a `builtin.conflict` opens "The task changed while you were editing it" with theirs and yours, "Keep mine" re-sending it over theirs; a refusal of any other kind is a band in the card. Escape in a dialog opened from the card closes only that dialog (`ConfirmDialog` stops `cancel`, which React passes up its tree). A subtask's rail in the chain has the parent's key and title as its tip (`t.chain.family`), a press on it opening the parent in the "Parents" view, and a backlog row of a subtask shows `↳ <parent key>`. Lists opened from the card are at most 420 px wide, a long title ending in "…". The refusals are in `docs/trackers/builtin.md` ("The built-in tracker"); the fake is `?card=builtin|conflict` (`docs/web/fake-states.md`).
- The card's subtasks (BTN-1152, `Subtasks` in `CardTree.tsx`): a task whose card gives `subtasks` (every tracker but Forgejo, `docs/trackers.md`) shows "Subtasks" with the closed and all counts under the description, its direct children in the chain's order (`chainOrdered`: the chain's ones first, the rest by number), each a key that opens its card with its status beside it. On any tracker but the built-in one the list is read-only and a task without subtasks shows no block. In the fake, a parent of the "Parents" view (`ABC-16`, `ABC-17`) has a card with the fake's tasks under it.
- The journal in the card (BTN-774, `CardJournal.tsx`, the pure rules in `journalView.ts`, styles in `cardMain.css`): every tracker's comments go newest first (BTN-1451; before it oldest first, as the trackers show them), the latest 20 shown and "N more" under them showing the older rest, the chat's task panel too; the session's chat itself keeps a messenger's order, the newest at the bottom. A built-in task's section is "Journal" with its count, its entries named "You" (the person's notes, framed in the accent), "Agent" with the model as a `RunLabelTag` beside the name (`journalModel`, once the comment carries it; BTN-1112) or "Even Keel" with its mark; another tracker's comment keeps its author. A built-in task's changes (BTN-1112, `CardChanges.tsx`, `cardChanges.css`, `journalItems`/`changeRows`) go into the same stream by time: no bubble, a small author circle on the comments' avatar axis, the head "who, when", then a framed table on `--bg` with alternating `--stripe` rows — the field's name left of a line, "before → after" right of it; a list field (labels, links, subtasks) one row of "+ name" and struck-out chips; statuses as dots, the old one muted; a description "Changed" with "Show difference" folding out red and green lines and "⋯ N lines" for an unchanged stretch. One author's changes within 5 minutes of the group's first are one entry (`groupChanges`), and the window of 20 counts entries after the filter. The heading's segmented filter All / Comments / Changes starts at All on each open. The replies in a comment's thread (BTN-909, `TaskComment.replies`, only Huly gives them) stand under its bubble, indented by the avatar's column with a line at their left, oldest first (`repliesOf`), never folded and not counted in the heading, with a smaller avatar and the same author and time; the chat's task panel shows them too. Only for a built-in task: a trash button on the person's own note asks and sends `builtin/uncomment`; an empty journal says what will appear in it; the note field right under the heading and its filter, above the entries (BTN-1451), sends `builtin/comment` on Enter (Shift+Enter a new line; on a touch screen Enter is a new line and the send button sends), with a line under it that a note does not reach the session — its questions are answered in the session's chat. Closing the card asks nothing: an unsent note stays as the field's draft and is back in it on the next open, until it is sent or the field emptied. The fake is `?card=builtin&journal=empty|long|asked|history|history-long` (`docs/web/fake-states.md`).
- The description editor (BTN-772, a built-in task's card; `CardDescription.tsx` in the card's chunk, `DescriptionEditor.tsx` in its own lazy chunk with TipTap — ~519 kB, ~163 kB gzip, loaded only when a description is edited; the pure Markdown side in `descriptionDoc.ts`, `descriptionLoss.ts`, `descriptionPaste.ts`; styles in `description.css`): "Edit" by the heading (its tip says a double click on the text opens it too) turns the description into the editor; the text is always Markdown, and the Preview/Markdown switch (a `Segmented`, remembered in `even-keel.description.look`) only changes how it is edited. Preview is TipTap with a toolbar (bold, italic, strike, code, H2, H3, lists, a checklist, a quote, a code block, a table, a link typed in a field of its own); it writes the text only on a real edit, so opening and closing it changes nothing, and an edit undone back is no change. The visual editor is off, with the reason as the switch's tip, when it would lose a part of the text: HTML, a footnote, a picture or anything it would write back otherwise (`previewLoss`, which compares the text with its round trip through the editor's schema); a table is written unpadded (`compactTable`). Markdown is a plain textarea; HTML pasted into it becomes Markdown (`htmlToMarkdown`). A file pasted (Ctrl+V) or dropped into either view becomes the task's attachment (BTN-773, `useUploads.ts`, the pure side in `descriptionUpload.ts`): one over the "Attachment size limit, MB" setting is refused before the upload with the `builtin.fileTooLarge` band and its button to Settings → Interface; otherwise an "uploading…" mark goes in at the cursor and is replaced by `![name](../url)` for a raster picture or `[name](../url)` for anything else once the upload answers. The visual editor has no picture node, so a file in Preview switches to Markdown (not remembered) and the mark goes at the end. A drag over the editor shows the drop overlay; Save is off, with the hint saying so, while an upload pends. Ctrl+Enter saves in both (before ProseMirror's own Mod-Enter), Escape leaves, asking first when there are changes, and Escape in that question closes only the question. Saving sends `builtin/edit` with `field: 'description'`, the text the editor started from and the new one, and nothing when they are equal. A `builtin.conflict` opens "The description changed while you were editing it": the new version against yours line by line (`DiffLines`, shared with the changelog), the count of the lines keeping yours would overwrite, "Keep mine" re-sending yours over theirs and "Take the new one" reopening the editor on theirs, with yours kept under it ("Your version": "Copy", "Put mine back") until the card closes. Closing the card (×, Escape, the backdrop) or the tab with the editor open saves the changes; hiding the tab saves them and keeps the editor open; only the editor's Cancel (and Escape inside it) drops them. The fake is `?card=builtin&description=html|long|empty` and `?card=conflict` (`docs/web/fake-states.md`).
- Drafts (BTN-1027, `cardDrafts.ts`, `useLeaving.ts`): what the person typed into a built-in task's title, description or note is kept in `localStorage` under `even-keel.draft.<project GUID>.<task>.<field>` as `{ from, to }` from the moment it is sent until the service takes it; any refusal keeps it. `useLeaving` saves on `visibilitychange` → hidden, `pagehide` and unmount; a save sent as the page goes away is a `keepalive` request when its body fits 60 000 bytes (`fitsKeepalive`, the browser caps all in flight at 64 KiB), a plain one otherwise, which may not arrive. On the next open of the card a draft whose `to` the field does not hold yet is sent again over its own `from` (once per mount: React's strict mode runs a mount effect twice), so a field changed meanwhile opens the same conflict window; "Take the new one", Escape on the title and Cancel in the editor drop the draft.
- Body limits (BTN-1183): the server takes 64 KiB of JSON per request, but the built-in tracker's commands that carry a task's text (`builtin/create`, `edit`, `description`, `patch`, `comment`; `BUILTIN_BODY_LIMITS` in `src/builtin/routes.ts`) take 4 MiB, since the card's `edit` sends the old and the new description together. Past a limit the refusal `request.tooLarge` names it and says to shorten the text or attach a part as a file.
- The card's attachments (`Attachments.tsx`, styles in `media.css`): every tracker's files are tiles — a picture, a video with its play mark, anything else its extension — each with its name (cut with "…", whole in the tip) and size under it. Only a built-in task's tile (BTN-773, the card carries `edit` and the file its `id`) has a trash button on its corner, shown on hover or focus and always on a touch screen; it asks "Delete the attachment?" (a link to it in the description stops opening) and sends `builtin/detach`. A record whose file is gone from the service's disk is a "No file" tile with the reason as its tip, still deletable. The fake is `?card=builtin&files=missing`.
- Adding tasks (BTN-770, a project a tracker view of kind `builtin` lists; `AddGhost.tsx`, `addGhost.css`, the pure rules in `web/src/ui/addGhost.ts`, the project's milestones with their status from `builtin/milestones` in `useProjectMilestones.ts`): a dashed, faded "+ Add task to the milestone <name>" (in the group with no milestone "+ Add task") ends every chain group that shows its rows — not a folded one, not the archive, not a group the "Project tasks" rules shut out (`takesGhost`: a milestone `ownsMilestone` accepts, tasks with no milestone only when the rules let them in). A task made there is Todo with the group's milestone, so it enters the chain at once; while the chain runs, the ghost's tip says it is taken in its turn. A planned or active milestone with no task yet (`emptyMilestones`, `milestoneView`'s `empty`) is an empty group with only its ghost, placed by the milestones' order; a completed or canceled one without open tasks is not shown, its closed tasks are in the archive (BTN-1462); a project with no task at all has one "+ Add task" as its whole empty state (`looseGhost`); while the chain has no open task that ghost asks in the accent (`invite`, `.add-ghost--invite`: accent text and dashed edge on an 8% accent wash), and its hover makes the edge solid and the wash 18%, so the asking and the pointer stay apart. The Backlog tab's count of such a project stands at 0 too, and the panel has "+ Add task" on top: Backlog, no milestone, the new row unfolding under the ghost (`backlog__row--new`). A press (mouse, Enter, Space) turns the ghost into a title field of its height in the same place. Enter sends `builtin/create` (`project`, `title`, `status`, `milestone`) and keeps the field open and empty for the next one; Ctrl+Enter also opens the new task's card; Escape closes it, keeping its text as that ghost's draft (`draftKey`, in memory until the page reloads, never shared between tabs); leaving it closes only an empty one. A blank title creates nothing and says so under the field; a refusal stays there as its code's text with the title kept; a second Enter before the answer sends nothing. The fake is `?tracker=builtin` (`docs/web/fake-states.md`).
