# The project page: the chain and the backlog

The chain's rows and their marks, folding and the archive, long milestones, a task's way into the chain and back, the drag, the backlog's order and search, and the feed's other views. How a change moves on screen is in `motion.md`.

## A chain row

### Tiers and the number

`web/src/ui/look.ts`, `web/src/pages/chain.css`. The rows come in three tiers: the current task is a two-line lead card with the session's last action (`showsAction`: any other task's session shows it too while mid-turn, as when the chain is stopped under a working session), the next three are full rows, the rest and the closed ones are compact — the **far rows**.

- The number's shape is the priority: high — filled, medium — ring, low — faded, urgent — a still red "!" in the number's place, the place in the chain in its tip (BTN-1162). The current task's number is ▶; while its session has a cache, the cache ring stands there instead (below), as on a row without a number. The Legend (`#help`) explains priority numbers separately from state lamps.
- "Rework" hatches the row; a question label cuts the corner amber — the row's only sign of it, with no chip (BTN-1153), the corner's tip saying what the label means.
- The row shows no tracker status word (BTN-615): the lamp tells the state, and the task card names the status. A skipped task keeps its "skipped" chip on the row, since the lamp may be showing a question, but "Restore" is only in its session panel, whose button says in its tip that the task is skipped. A closed task with work left keeps its "Done" button on the row.
- The blue frame belongs only to the current task; the row open in the panel (a chain block or an archive row) gets an accent fill instead, and only keyboard focus draws an outline.
- A press anywhere on a row lands on its key: a chain task's opens its session panel, a locked blocker's (any kind outside the chain) and a backlog row's open the task card, the only place that links to the tracker; titles are plain text.

### The lamp (`taskLamp` in `web/src/state.ts`)

One for every place the task shows — the chain block, the home card, the queue, the blockers list: the tracker status first (done, canceled), then the session.

- With no session a task in the backlog (status category `UnStarted`) is `backlog` (dotted ring), not `ahead`, so a blocker the chain does not show reads as waiting in the backlog.
- A finished session of an open task is `stopped` (amber ring with a stop square), not done; a session whose last line is `CHECKPOINT: …` (`SessionView.checkpoint`) is `handoff` (blue arrow) whether its process lives or not, and it does not count as waiting for a reply.
- A background job that `claude agents` still calls `working` but has no live process 5 min after its launch is finished (`lampOf` in `src/sessions/agents.ts`).
- Working has a triangle, waiting a question mark, and a completed branch fix a cross, so the marks differ without relying on colour (`ui/Lamp.tsx`, `ui/lamp.css`). Fix and release-carry rows show the same lamp beside their key, the archive included; their Forge smiths mark the service agent, with crossed hammers on a completed fix.

### Links: the lock and the key

`taskLinks` in `web/src/state.ts`, `web/src/ui/Links.tsx` (`Links` with `stack`, BTN-1365).

- A lock counts the open tasks the row waits for, a key the open ones waiting for it; a pointed counter lists them in a popover (top layer, so the scrolling panel does not clip it), and a task there opens its card.
- A zero is not drawn and keeps no place. The two stand in one narrow column at the row's right edge, the lock above the key, centred on the row's height and out of the flow, so they never make a row taller, the lowest 34 px far rows included; a row with one of them has it in the middle. A row without links has no column and its labels reach the edge; a row with any makes room for the column (`padding-right`), so the column keeps the alignment down the chain.
- Each mark keeps a wider hover and focus area than its small icon and figure. A phone's row tail (`BlockTail`) stacks them the same way, without figures.
- A backlog card (BTN-1386) takes the same stack, a child of the card rather than of its meta line, centred on the meta and the title together, on a phone too: a card whose title or meta wraps keeps the two marks one tight stack in its middle.
- The rows related to the pointed one, or else the open one (or the dragged one), keep their frame and light a counter in blue: a blocker its key, a task waiting on it its lock — one task's relations at a time, never two mixed.

### A live session's metrics (`web/src/ui/BlockMetrics.tsx`, BTN-299, BTN-331)

Each part has its figures in a `Tipped` tip:

- the cache as a ring that drains to cold (`cacheShare`; green, amber in its last fifth, a dashed grey ring once cold). It takes the number's place when the row is current or has no number, and stands in the plate otherwise;
- the context with its percent (blue, amber, red by the `context` thresholds);
- the account's initial in its colour, only when the session runs on an account other than the project's (`foreignAccount` in `web/src/accounts.ts`: no folder with that e-mail lists the project in `AccountEntry.projects`);
- the context resets, hidden at 0. The resets (`contextResets` in `web/src/state.ts`) count every new session with a handoff — checkpoint, limit, another cause of a `relay`, a restart by hand — and every compaction of the task's sessions; the tip breaks them down, and a session's row in the panel's session list says how it was launched and adds its compactions. Compactions come from the transcripts the monitor follows (`TranscriptSummary.compactions`: Claude's `compact_boundary`, Codex's `compacted` line, `docs/unverified.md`), kept per session in memory (`SessionMonitor.compactionsOf`) and put on `TaskSession.compactions`, so a session gone from the agents' list before the service started shows none.

A row whose session opens its second line (`showsAction`: working, and the lead's also on an error or a limit) gives the title the whole first line and puts `ActionMeter` under it, as wide as the title and the side: the current action (`ActionScreen`, an empty prompt while there is none; an API error or a limit is the action until the session acts again, in red with "!") on a field filled to the context level with a hard edge (`--fill`, registered so a new level slides), then the initial, the resets and the percent; it opens with `action-open` as one piece. Any other metered row stays one line with the metrics as a plate before the links; a later chip of the row goes on its end.

### Families and blockers

- **Family rails.** Subtasks of one parent are tied by a coloured rail left of the rows, dotted through the tasks between them, up to three lanes; on a phone the rails stand in the page's 16 px margin, off the rows (BTN-1162). Each row draws its own piece (`RailLink`), reaching past its border to meet the next row's across the gap; the first row of "Under way" stands the caption's height lower, so its piece reaches that much further up and the rail runs on through the frame (BTN-1651). A card taken by a drag hides its own piece, the other rows keep theirs, and it comes back in the new order once the drop's glide is over (`dropped` of `useChainDrag`, `SETTLE_MS`). A press on a rail (a wider hit strip over the 2 px rail) opens the parent in the parents view.
- **A locked blocker** — a task outside the chain that holds a chain task — stands in by `--blocker-indent` (`chain.css`; never more, a run of them is no ladder). Each run of blockers, the locked rows of the chain and the holders of "Outside the chain" alike, is tied to the task it stands before by a bracket (BTN-1714, `web/src/ui/Bracket.tsx`, the path in `bracketPath.ts`): a thin solid accent line halfway in the indent from the topmost blocker's middle with a rounded corner down to the top edge of the held task, straight ticks to the other blockers, no arrow. It is one SVG path per list, the last item of `.chain__blocks`, drawn from the rows' layout boxes (`offsetLeft`/`offsetTop`) on every render and every size change of the list or a row (`ResizeObserver`), not pieces of the rows' borders, which would overlap at the seams; it hides while a drag moves the rows. It stands inside the rows' column, so the family rails never meet it.

## Folding and the archive

`web/src/pages/ChainList.tsx`, `ChainDivider.tsx`.

- The divider line itself is the button. A hidden milestone is a dashed line that reveals it; the header of a revealed one folds it together with those after it. Milestones up to the first one with open tasks cannot be folded (`fixed` in `web/src/ui/milestones.ts`); a chain without milestones has no lines at all, except the archive's.
- The line's counts glue no words (BTN-1374): the archive's closed tasks are a check mark with a grey `.count-badge`, its fixes of main crossed hammers with theirs, a kind with none left out; a hidden milestone's open tasks are a struck eye with the badge; the words ("4 tasks", "Hidden, 3 tasks") are each mark's `title`.
- The archive is one line above the chain: folded, a dashed line with the count that opens the latest page; open, a block of its own (`archiveView` gives its pages apart from the chain, `archived`) — the line "N of M" with "K more" for the next, older page and a press on the line folding it all, and under it the closed tasks as quiet one-line rows (`ArchivedRow`: lamp, key, title, the context resets (`ResetsBadge`), the milestone only where it changes) in the columns of the chain's rows, never inside the milestone sections. The last closed task stays a row of the chain.
- Older pages (BTN-1445, `useArchivePages.ts`, `docs/chain/entry.md`): "M" counts the state's closed tasks plus `archiveMore`; a press that needs more rows than the state holds asks `archive/page` through `read`, not `query`, so the page's loader stays still and the pressed button spins instead (`aria-busy` on `.chain__toggle` or `.chain__more`). The server's rows stand above the state's closed ones (`archiveView`'s `older`); a `next` of `null` ends the archive even when the count says more. A link to a task neither in the chain nor loaded (`#BTN-300`, `?task=`) asks `archive/page` with `focus` once per id, and the archive unfolds down to it.

## Long milestones (BTN-1188, `milestonePage` in `web/src/ui/chainPage.ts`)

- Each open milestone section — and a chain without milestones as one — draws at most `CHAIN_PAGE` (50) rows, the backlog's ghost not counted. Under the 50th a row of the chain's width and a far row's height (`.chain__page-more`, neither a task nor the dashed add ghost) says "Show 16 more of 35" ("Show the remaining 12 tasks" for the last step); each press adds `CHAIN_MORE` (16) rows of that milestone.
- The presses are kept per milestone name and forgotten when the milestone is folded, so a milestone opened again shows its first 50.
- A row that must stay in view (`staysInView`: the current task, a live session, a question, an error) never hides: past the cut it stands under the button with its own number, out of the button's count. Numbers run through the whole chain, not the drawn part.
- A task the address focuses opens as many steps as it needs; a ghost or an arrival from the backlog landing behind the button is not drawn, scrolled to or marked, so the page stays still.
- A drop puts the dragged task right after the row it was let go under (`droppedOrder`): dropped under the last row before the button, it lands ahead of the hidden ones, which keep their order.

## Into the chain and back (BTN-509)

The tracker side is in `docs/chain/entry.md`, "Entry into the chain"; the task card shows the same two buttons under its facts.

- **"Into the chain"** (`readyButton` in `web/src/state.ts`) stands for a backlog task in `UnStarted` where the tracker has `ready`, and for one that lacks a required label (`ProjectOrder.requiredLabel` is `false`) where it has `relabel` — so a GitHub or GitLab issue without the label gets it too. It is off with the reason as its title when the task is in `ProjectOrder.refused`.
- **"To the backlog"** (`canUnready`) stands for a chain task in `ToDo` or `Active` where the tracker has `unready`, or one carrying a required label where it has `relabel`, and never while a session of the task is live (`hasLiveSession`). In the chain it is `.block__unready` (an arrow out of a box, `OutOfChainIcon`) right before the row's link counters, shown on hover or focus within the row and always on a device without hover.
- A built-in tracker's card has neither: its status pick does the same (`task-card.md`).
- **The landing** — the place the service says a task would take in the chain (`ProjectOrder.landing`; `landingIndex` puts it ahead of the locked blockers of the task it precedes). While a backlog row's "Into the chain" is pointed at (for 250 ms, so a pointer passing over the buttons does not shake the chain) or pressed, a ghost block stands there in the size of the tier it will take and the page scrolls to it, revealing a folded milestone (`web/src/pages/useArrival.ts`). A locked blocker's "Into the chain" on its chain row (BTN-1642) has the same ghost, right under its own locked row when it lands before the task it holds (`landingIndex`'s `own`), in the section of the row it stands before; the page does not scroll but keeps the row under the pointer (`useLockedEntry`). A section that comes in above the open milestones — a task without a milestone landing among them — does not fold the last open one. The flight from the backlog is in `motion.md`.

## The drag

- A preview and an optimistic drop share `withOrder` (`web/src/ui/chainOrder.ts`): `withWaiting` places each locked blocker before its first dependent in the proposed order, and the fix and carry rows hold to the neighbour `chainBlocks` put them by (BTN-808): a fix right above the task it interrupted, a carry asked for right after the interrupted task, else the current one; a fix that interrupted nothing and a running carry stay on top.
- `pinnedHead` keeps every current or interrupted task above the dragged row — every running lane's, as one group (BTN-1611) — above it, so a drop lands no higher than right after the last of them. A task the chain passed over between running ones (pinned to a busy machine, BTN-1643) stays where it is and moves only under the running one above it (the server's `jumpsAhead` keeps the same rule), and `reorderable` does not let any of them be picked up. A drop never lands inside the group "Under way" or over it.
- `useChainDrag` measures every displayed row for `stackedTops`, locked blockers and closed rows included; `dragOffsets` moves the rows other than the dragged one only up or down, so a locked blocker keeps its indent in any place and a row taking its place keeps none (BTN-1767). Drop targets and the submitted order hold only chain tasks.
- The card lifts (scale 1.02 and a shadow) when taken and settles back when let go, both through `--motion-fast`, with no motion under reduced motion.
- A drop the service refuses (BTN-1644) is a toast in the page's corner, `ChainRefusalToast` through the `chainRefusal` store: the dragged task "stays where it was", the refusal with its advice under it, gone by itself after `TOAST_MS`; the band under the list's last row is only the row buttons' errors. The rows go back when the drop's own glide is over (`afterDrop` of `useChainDrag`), so `useRowGlide` glides them home as any reorder.

## The backlog

- **Order** (BTN-934, `web/src/pages/backlogOrder.ts`): three segments in the panel's head — the newest on top (the default), the oldest on top, and by priority (urgent to none, the newest first within one). A task's number stands for its age: the model has no creation date and numbers only grow on every tracker. The page sorts; the service sends the backlog by number (`byNumber`). The choice is kept in the browser (`even-keel.backlog.order`), one for every project. The built-in tracker's add ghost stays first in any order; a new task unfolds at its sorted place, and a row leaving for the chain slides out from its place in the sorted list.
- **A row** shows priority bars, the key with the icon button "Into the chain" left of the whole row, the milestone and the counters. Its meta line shows the task's run from `state.runs` (the card's "Runs on", BTN-983) by `RunLabelTag` right after its key and parent; the run and the milestone shrink to a small least before their text goes (the milestone's name giving way first), and a row whose least does not fit wraps its work chip (`.backlog__tail`) to a second line instead of cutting the run.
- **Search and pages** (BTN-982, `web/src/pages/backlogSearch.ts`): a field under the hint filters what is already on the page: case aside, each word of the query must be somewhere in the key, the title, the run's text (`taskRunText`) or the milestone's name; the order stays the sort's, the head adds "found X of N", nothing found is one muted line. Escape with a query clears it, without one closes the panel; a closed panel forgets the query. The list shows the first `BACKLOG_PAGE` (50) rows after the search and the sort and a "N more" button that adds 50; a change of the query goes back to the first 50.
- **Text changed**: a task whose outsider author changed its text after the entry label (`ProjectOrder.changed`, BTN-1124/BTN-1135, `docs/chain/entry.md`) shows an amber "text changed" chip after its key and parent; its tip says to read the task and press "Into the chain", which puts the label on again.

## The feed's views (BTN-1041, BTN-1428)

`web/src/parents.ts`, `web/src/pages/FeedSwitch.tsx`. A `.segmented` of icons — the chain in order, the parents as a tree (only once the project has a task with subtasks), the tasks outside the chain as a crossed-out eye — stands at the right of the feed's top row, the archive line's, or alone there; on a narrow screen in the head beside the tools. The view is the address (`?view=parents`, `?view=outside`; `feedView`, `parentsTo`/`outsideTo`/`chainTo`), so "back" and links keep it; without parents `view=parents` is ignored. While another view is shown, the chain's button has a dot when the chain is paused or its current task waits for a reply (`chainCalls`); the head's chip stays as it is. Each view keeps its own last frame for the chain's motion.

### The parents view

The parents come from the server in `EvenKeelState.parents` (`ParentTask`: a task with `subtasks` and `closedSubtasks`; the open ones of the read, the closed ones the read lists, and those a chain session turned into parents), any status. `parentBlocks` makes them rows of the same `ChainList` — closed first by number for the archive, open by the chain's milestones then number — without a number, a drag, ghosts or the into/out of the chain buttons, and with a chip `closed/all` in the family's rail colour (`familyHue`). A row opens its panel as in the chain, the address keeping the view (`rowSearch`); the panel only reads: transcript and the task's sessions, no chat, Skip or New session, and its transcript window says why the field is shut (`WindowTask.parent`, `t.session.chat.parent`): an answer with a cold cache would offer a new session, which would run an agent on the parent.

### Outside the chain (BTN-1428)

`web/src/pages/OutsideView.tsx`, loaded on the first switch to it; the pure rules in `web/src/ui/outside.ts`. The project's open tasks that neither the chain nor the backlog shows, which otherwise appear nowhere on the page.

- **The read.** The view asks the server once when it opens: `outside/tasks {project}` (`outsideTasks` in `src/chain/outside.ts`) reads each of the project's readers anew by the tracker's status categories alone (`terminal`/`active` unset), since the regular read drops a milestone the rules leave out (`ownsMilestone`) and reads a status the rules count as closed as closed. It gives the open tasks without subtasks with their reason (`OutsideTask`, `outsideReason` in `src/chain/rules.ts`): an excluding label, a milestone the rules leave out, a status the rules count as closed while the tracker's category is open; a task with several goes under the first. Canceled (`Lost`) and done tasks are never in it. The page drops what the chain (its order and its locked blockers) and the backlog already show. It reads both anew, without the loader, after every edit in the task card (`TaskCard`'s `onChanged`) and after each read of the project's tracker (`TrackerView.readAt`), so a changed status moves the row at once. A failed read is a red line with "Read again"; nothing outside — a line saying every open task is in the chain or the backlog.
- **Groups.** Up to three in that order, each a `Divider` with the reason and its count that folds (`FoldBody`, only an open group draws its rows); "Excluding label" and "Status the rules leave out" open by default, "Milestone the rules leave out" folded (mostly old milestones' leftovers), the choice kept per project on this device (`even-keel.outside.open.<project>`). A group shows at most 50 rows across its milestones and then "Show more", 16 a press, as a long milestone.
- **Inside a group** the milestones go in the chain's milestone order (the project's saved order, then `milestoneComparator`), no milestone last, each an unfoldable heading with its count; under it the tasks by status (BTN-1673, `compareStatuses`: the category's order, Active first, and within one In Review, In Progress, Todo, Backlog, then other names), each status a small heading with its `StatusDot`, name and count. The rows are far rows without a number or a milestone: the lamp is the status's dot with its name in the tip. Within a status the rows go by the view's own `OrderSwitch` (`web/src/pages/OrderSwitch.tsx`, the backlog panel's switch too): newest first, oldest first or by priority (`compareTasks`), kept in `even-keel.outside.order`, apart from the backlog's. A row only opens the card; no "Into the chain": the label or the milestone are changed in the card.
- **Holders** (BTN-1714, `chainHolders`): before a task stand the open chain tasks among its `blockedBy`, in the chain's order, a closed one and a locked row left out — `HolderRow`, a dashed row in by the blockers' indent with the tracker status's dot and the status in words at the right, accent while its session works; its key opens its session panel in the chain (`chainTo`), a double click its card. They are not counted in the group, milestone or status counts and take no place of the group's page; the run before a task gets the blockers' bracket.
- **Archive** (BTN-1673): in the top row with the switches, folded, drawn only while it has any — the closed tasks an excluding label or a milestone the rules leave out kept out of the chain. `outside/archive {project, after}` (`outsideArchivePage`, on the chain archive's `closedPages` with `terminal`/`active` unset) pages the readers' `closedPage` the last closed first and keeps the leaves whose `outsideReason` is `label` or `milestone`; a label-excluded task of a milestone the chain takes is in the chain's archive too. Its line counts what is read ("10+ tasks" while older pages are left); unfolded it lists the rows as the chain's archive does, with "older" for the next page, kept per project (`even-keel.outside.archive.<project>`).

The fake states: `?backlog=links|many|changed`, `?chain=long|drag`, `?view=outside`, `?view=parents` (with `chain=paused` for the dot) in `docs/web/fake-states/chain-states.md`.
