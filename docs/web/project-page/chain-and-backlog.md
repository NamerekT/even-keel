# The project page: the chain and the backlog

Links on a row, folding, long milestones, entry into the chain, the backlog's order and search, the lamp, the feed's views.

- A task's links on a chain row (`Links` with `stack`, BTN-1365): the lock (blockers) above the key (dependents) in one narrow column at the row's right edge, centred on its height and out of the flow, so it never makes a row taller, the lowest 34 px rows of the far end included; a row with one of them has it in the middle. A zero is not drawn and keeps no place: a row without links has no column and its labels reach the edge, a row with any makes room for the column (`padding-right`), so the column keeps the alignment down the chain. The marks are small (10 px icon, 11 px figure); each keeps a wider hover and focus area than its mark, which opens its list of tasks as before. A phone's row tail (`BlockTail`) stacks them the same way, without figures. A backlog card (BTN-1386) takes the same stack, a child of the card rather than of its meta line, at the card's right edge and centred on the meta and the title together, on a phone too: a card whose title runs to several lines or whose meta wraps keeps the two marks one tight stack in its middle, never spread over its height; a card without links has no column, a card with any gives it its room (`padding-right`) on both lines. `dev:fake` with `?backlog=links` shows a lock alone, a lock and a key on a tall card, and a card without links.
- Folding on the project page (`web/src/pages/ChainList.tsx`): the divider line itself is the button. A hidden milestone is a dashed line that reveals it; the header of a revealed one folds it together with those after it. The line's count glues nothing (BTN-1374, `ChainDivider.tsx`): the archive's closed tasks are a check mark with a grey `.count-badge`, its fixes of main crossed hammers with theirs, a kind with none left out; a hidden milestone's open tasks are a struck eye with the badge; the words ("4 tasks", "Hidden, 3 tasks") are each mark's `title`. The archive is one line above the chain: folded, a dashed line with the count that opens the latest page; open, a block of its own (`archiveView` gives its pages apart from the chain, `archived`) — the line "N of M" with "K more" for the next, older page and a press on the line folding it all, and under it the closed tasks as quiet one-line rows (`ArchivedRow`: lamp, key, title, the context resets as on a chain row (`ResetsBadge`), the milestone only where it changes) in the columns of the chain's rows, never inside the milestone sections. The last closed task stays a row of the chain. Older pages (BTN-1445, `useArchivePages.ts`, `docs/chain/entry.md`): "M" counts the state's closed tasks plus `archiveMore`; a press that needs more rows than the state holds asks `archive/page` through `read`, not `query`, so the page's loader stays still and the pressed button spins instead (`aria-busy` on `.chain__toggle`, whose spinner takes the chevron's place, or on `.chain__more`, whose text it replaces). The server's rows stand above the state's closed ones (`archiveView`'s `older`); a `next` of `null` ends the archive even when the count says more. A link to a task neither in the chain nor loaded (`#BTN-300`, `?task=`) asks `archive/page` with `focus` once per id, and the archive unfolds down to it as for a closed task of the state. Milestones up to the first one with open tasks cannot be folded (`fixed` in `web/src/ui/milestones.ts`); a chain without milestones has no lines at all, except the archive's.
- Long milestones (BTN-1188, `milestonePage` in `web/src/ui/chainPage.ts`): each open milestone section — and a chain without milestones as one — draws at most `CHAIN_PAGE` (50) rows, the backlog's ghost not counted; under the 50th a row of the chain's width, a far row's height (44 px on a phone), filled with a 10% accent mix and a solid accent edge so it is neither a task nor the dashed add ghost (`.chain__page-more`), says "Show 16 more of 35" ("Show the remaining 12 tasks" for the last step), and each press adds `CHAIN_MORE` (16) rows of that milestone. The presses are kept per milestone name and forgotten when the milestone is folded, so a milestone opened again shows its first 50. A row that must stay in view (`staysInView`: the current task, a live session, a question, an error) never hides: past the cut it stands under the button with its own number, out of the button's count. Numbers run through the whole chain, not the drawn part. A task the address focuses opens as many steps as it needs; a ghost or an arrival from the backlog landing behind the button is not drawn, scrolled to or marked, so the page stays still. A drop puts the dragged task right after the row it was let go under (`droppedOrder`): dropped under the last row before the button, it lands ahead of the hidden ones, which keep their order. `?chain=long` in `dev:fake` adds 80 tasks to ABC's first milestone, the 66th with a question.
- A backlog task on its way into the chain (`web/src/pages/useArrival.ts`): while its "Into the chain" button is pointed at (for 250 ms, so a pointer passing over the buttons does not shake the chain) or pressed, a ghost block stands where it would land (`ProjectOrder.landing` from the service, `landingIndex` puts it ahead of the locked blockers of the task it precedes) and the page scrolls to it, revealing a folded milestone; the ghost, in the size of the tier it will take, and a milestone it unfolds grow open and fold shut by height instead of appearing at once. On the press the backlog row slides straight left out of the panel, fading, and folds (`.backlog__row--leaving`; the panel keeps a copy of it until then, should the chain take the task sooner); once it has gone and the task is in the chain, a copy enters at the height of its block from behind the side column (`.flight` stands under `.project__side`) and slides straight left onto it, fading in, so it does not pop up out of nowhere when the column is closed or another card is opened meanwhile (`Flight.tsx`); then the block is marked for a moment. On a narrow screen (≤760 px) and with reduced motion there is no flight: the narrow screen closes the panel instead. A locked blocker's "Into the chain" on its chain row (BTN-1642) has the same ghost, right under its own locked row when it lands before the task it holds (`landingIndex`'s `own`), in the section of the row it stands before; the page does not scroll to it but keeps the row under the pointer (`useLockedEntry`). On the press nothing flies: once the task is in the chain its own row slides from where it stood to its place over `--motion-step` and is marked. A section that comes in above the open milestones — a task without a milestone landing among them — does not fold the last open one.
- Into the chain and back (BTN-509, `docs/chain/entry.md`, "Entry into the chain"): "Into the chain" (`readyButton` in `web/src/state.ts`) stands for a backlog task in `UnStarted` where the tracker has `ready`, and for one that lacks a required label (`ProjectOrder.requiredLabel` is `false`) where it has `relabel` — so a GitHub or GitLab issue without the label gets it too; it is off with the reason as its title when the task is in `ProjectOrder.refused`. "To the backlog" (`canUnready`) stands for a chain task in `ToDo` or `Active` where the tracker has `unready`, or one carrying a required label where it has `relabel`, and never while a session of the task is live (`hasLiveSession`): in the card under the facts, and in the chain as `.block__unready` (an arrow out of a box, `OutOfChainIcon`) right before the row's link counters, shown on hover or focus within the row and always on a device without hover.
- The backlog's order (BTN-934, `web/src/pages/backlogOrder.ts`): three segments in the panel's head, by the title — the newest on top (the default), the oldest on top, and by priority (urgent to none, the newest first within one). A task's number stands for its age: the model has no creation date and numbers only grow on every tracker. The page sorts; the service still sends the backlog by number (`byNumber`). The choice is kept in the browser (`even-keel.backlog.order`), one for every project, as Home's grouping. The built-in tracker's add ghost stays first in any order; a new task unfolds at its sorted place, and a row leaving for the chain slides out from its place in the sorted list.
- The backlog's search, pages and runs (BTN-982, `web/src/pages/backlogSearch.ts`): a row's meta line shows the task's run from `state.runs` (the card's "Runs on", BTN-983) by `RunLabelTag` right after its key and parent; the run and the milestone grow from a small least up to their text (the run far faster, the milestone's name giving way first), and a row whose least does not fit wraps its work chip (`.backlog__tail`, drawn only when there is work) to a second line instead of cutting the run. A field under the hint filters on the page what is already there: case aside, each word of the query must be somewhere in the key, the title, the run's text (`taskRunText`) or the milestone's name; the order stays the sort's, the head adds "found X of N", nothing found is one muted line. Escape with a query clears it, without one closes the panel; the query is not kept, a closed panel forgets it. The list shows the first `BACKLOG_PAGE` (50) rows after the search and the sort and a "N more" button that adds 50; a change of the query goes back to the first 50. The add ghost stays first, and a row leaving for the chain slides out of the rows shown. `dev:fake` with `?backlog=many` adds 64 backlog tasks to ABC, every fifth with a run.
- A backlog task whose outsider author changed its text after the entry label (`ProjectOrder.changed`, BTN-1124/BTN-1135, `docs/chain/entry.md`) shows an amber "text changed" chip after its key and parent; its tip says to read the task and press "Into the chain", which puts the label on again. `dev:fake` with `?backlog=changed` marks ABC-19.
- The lamp of a task (`taskLamp` in `web/src/state.ts`) is one for every place the task shows — the chain block, the home card, the queue, the blockers list: the tracker status first (done, canceled), then the session; with no session a task in the backlog (status category `UnStarted`) is `backlog` (dotted ring), not `ahead`, so a blocker the chain does not show reads as waiting in the backlog. A finished session of an open task is `stopped` (amber ring with a stop square), not done; a session whose last line is `CHECKPOINT: …` (`SessionView.checkpoint`) is `handoff` (blue arrow) whether its process lives or not, and it does not count as waiting for a reply. A background job that `claude agents` still calls `working` but has no live process 5 min after its launch is finished (`lampOf` in `src/sessions/agents.ts`). Working has a triangle, waiting a question mark, and a completed branch fix a cross, so the marks differ without relying on colour (`ui/Lamp.tsx`, `ui/lamp.css`). Fix and release-carry rows also show the same state lamp beside their key, including in the archive; their Forge smiths identify the service agent, with crossed hammers on a completed fix. A task whose merge is being landed (`underway` of `chain.landings`, BTN-1779, `docs/chain/worktrees.md`) is lit at work with the tip "merging into the main branch" (`landingTasks` in `web/src/completion.ts`) on the row, the project card, the session panel and the machine page's sessions; the header's questions, the tab's dot and the README badge do not count it as waiting. Tests: `src/chain/landings-underway.test.ts`, `src/e2e/landing.test.ts` (a long `commands.check`).
- The fix of main's row (`docs/chain/main-ci.md`): in place of its lamp a forge (`web/src/pages/Forge.tsx`) in the lamp's colour, set right after the title "Fixing main" so it never meets the cache ring in the number column — two smiths take turns with hammers on a red-hot ingot (the red main) while the session works and stand with hammers down by a cooling one otherwise; with `prefers-reduced-motion`, two crossed hammers. A fix that is over is a closed row with the lamp `fixed` (purple, `--lamp-fix`) and crossed hammers, titled "Fix main" with its red and landed commits, "… — dropped", "… — nothing to fix, green on a rerun" or "… — fixed outside the chain"; the archive counts the fixes apart, the hammers' mark with its count beside the tasks' check mark.
- The carry's row (`docs/chain/projects.md`, "The carry session"): a block of the main project's chain like the fix; a closed carry has the lamp `done` and the forge's crossed hammers. Waiting for its turn, its panel (`CarryWait`) gives the Conflict fact a box per file — its path, its lines and a five-cell bar against the largest file, as `git diff --stat` draws it, or "deleted" for a file deleted on one side; eight files show, "N more files" opens the rest.
- The look of a chain block beyond its lamp (`web/src/ui/look.ts`, `web/src/pages/chain.css`): the current task is a two-line lead card with the session's last action (`showsAction`: any other task's session shows it too while mid-turn, as when the chain is stopped under a working session), the next three are full rows, the rest and the closed ones are compact; the number's shape is the priority (high — filled, medium — ring, low — faded, urgent — a still red "!" in the number's place, the place in the chain in its tip (BTN-1162; once a beacon and hazard tape), the current task — ▶); "Rework" hatches the row, a question label cuts the corner amber — the row's only sign of it, with no chip (BTN-1153), the corner's tip saying what the label means; subtasks of one parent are tied by a coloured rail left of the rows, dotted through the tasks between them, up to three lanes; on a phone the rails stand in the page's 16 px margin, off the rows, 4 px apart (BTN-1162). Each row draws its own piece (`RailLink`), reaching 4 px past its border to meet the next row's across the 8 px gap; the first row of "Under way" stands the caption's 8 px lower, so its piece reaches that much further up and the rail runs on through the frame (BTN-1651). A card taken by a drag hides its own piece, solid or dotted, the other rows keep theirs, and it comes back in the new order once the drop's glide is over (`dropped` of `useChainDrag`, `SETTLE_MS`); the card lifts (scale 1.02 and a shadow) when taken and settles back when let go, both through `--motion-fast`, with no motion under reduced motion. The Legend (`#help`) explains priority numbers separately from state lamps. The blue frame belongs only to the current task; the row open in the panel, a chain block or an archive row, gets an accent fill instead, and only keyboard focus draws an outline; the rows related to the pointed one, or else the open one (or the dragged one), keep their frame and light a counter in blue instead: a blocker its key, a task waiting on it its lock — one task's relations at a time, never two mixed. A press anywhere on a row lands on its key: a chain task's opens its session panel, a locked blocker's (any kind outside the chain) and a backlog row's open the task card, the only place that links to the tracker; titles are plain text. The row shows no tracker status word (BTN-615): the lamp tells the state, and the task card names the status. A skipped task keeps its "skipped" chip on the row, since the lamp may be showing a question, but "Restore" is only in its session panel, whose button says in its tip that the task is skipped; the panel has no "skipped" flag of its own. A closed task with work left keeps its "Done" button on the row. A locked blocker stands in by `--blocker-indent` (14 px, `chain.css`; never more, a run of them is no ladder), and each run of blockers, the locked rows of the chain and the holders of "Outside the chain" alike, is tied to the task it stands before by a bracket (BTN-1714, `web/src/ui/Bracket.tsx`, the path in `bracketPath.ts`): a thin solid accent line (1 px, never dashed) halfway in the indent, never left of the leftmost row, from the topmost blocker's middle with a rounded corner (6 px) down to the top edge of the held task, straight ticks to the other blockers, no arrow. It is one SVG path per list, the last item of the `.chain__blocks`, drawn from the rows' layout boxes (`offsetLeft`/`offsetTop`) on every render and on every size change of the list or a row (`ResizeObserver`), not pieces of the rows' borders, which would overlap at the seams; it hides while a drag moves the rows. It stands inside the rows' column, so the family rails left of the rows never meet it.
- The feed's views (BTN-1041, BTN-1428, `web/src/parents.ts`, `web/src/pages/FeedSwitch.tsx`): a `.segmented` of icons — the chain in order, the parents as a tree (only once the project has a task with subtasks), the tasks outside the chain as a crossed-out eye — stands at the right of the feed's top row, the archive line's, or alone there; on a narrow screen in the head beside the tools. The view is the address (`?view=parents`, `?view=outside`; `feedView`, `parentsTo`/`outsideTo`/`chainTo`), so "back" and links keep it; without parents `view=parents` is ignored. While another view is shown, the chain's button has a dot when the chain needs the user (below).
- "Outside the chain" (BTN-1428, `web/src/pages/OutsideView.tsx`, loaded on the first switch to it like the card; the pure rules in `web/src/ui/outside.ts`): the project's open tasks that neither the chain nor the backlog shows, which otherwise appear nowhere on the page. The view asks the server once when it opens: `outside/tasks {project}` (`outsideTasks` in `src/chain/outside.ts`) reads each of the project's readers anew by the tracker's status categories alone (`terminal`/`active` unset), since the regular read drops a milestone the rules leave out (`ownsMilestone`) and does not read a status the rules count as closed as open, and gives the open tasks without subtasks with their reason (`OutsideTask`, `outsideReason` in `src/chain/rules.ts`): an excluding label, a milestone the rules leave out, a status the rules count as closed while the tracker's category is open; a task with several goes under the first. Canceled (`Lost`) and done tasks are never in it. The page drops what the chain (its order and its locked blockers) and the backlog already show. Up to three groups in that order, each a `Divider` with the reason and its count that folds (`FoldBody`, only an open group draws its rows); "Excluding label" and "Status the rules leave out" open by default, "Milestone the rules leave out" folded (mostly old milestones' leftovers), the choice kept per project on this device (`localStorage` `even-keel.outside.open.<project>`). Inside a group the milestones in the chain's milestone order (the project's saved order, then `milestoneComparator`), no milestone last, each an unfoldable heading with a flag, its name, a line and its count; under it the tasks by status (BTN-1673, `compareStatuses`: the category's order, Active first, and within one In Review, In Progress, Todo, Backlog, then other names), each status a small heading with its `StatusDot`, name and count, and under it the rows as far rows of the chain without a number or a milestone: the lamp is the status's dot with its name in the tip, the right side is empty (no label tag since BTN-1673). Within a status the rows go by the view's own order, an `OrderSwitch` (`web/src/pages/OrderSwitch.tsx`, the backlog panel's switch too) left of the view switch while the view has rows: newest first (the default), oldest first or by priority (`compareTasks` in `web/src/pages/backlogOrder.ts`), kept on this device in `localStorage` `even-keel.outside.order`, apart from the backlog's. A group shows at most 50 rows across its milestones and then "Show more", 16 a press, as a long milestone (`milestonePage`), the rest going on under the same heading. A row only opens the card (its key, a double click); no "Into the chain": the label or the milestone are changed in the card. Before a task stand the open chain tasks among its `blockedBy` (BTN-1714, `chainHolders`: the page's chain rows, in the chain's order, a closed one and a locked row left out), one before each task it holds: `HolderRow`, a dashed transparent row in by the blockers' indent with the tracker status's dot and the status in words at the right, accent while its session works; its key opens its session panel in the chain (`chainTo`), a double click its card. They are not counted in the group, milestone or status counts and do not take a place of the group's page; the run before a task gets the blockers' bracket (below). A failed read is a red line with "Read again"; nothing outside — a line saying every open task is in the chain or the backlog. Above the groups, in the top row with the switches, the folded "Archive" (BTN-1673) of the closed tasks an excluding label or a milestone the rules leave out kept out of the chain, drawn only while it has any: `outside/archive {project, after}` (`outsideArchivePage` in `src/chain/outside.ts`, on the chain archive's `closedPages` with the rules' `terminal`/`active` unset) pages the readers' `closedPage` the last closed first and keeps the leaves whose `outsideReason` is `label` or `milestone`; a label-excluded task of a milestone the chain takes is in the chain's archive too. Its line counts what is read ("10+ tasks" while older pages are left), unfolded it lists the rows as the chain's archive does (lamp, key opening the card, the milestone where it changes) with "older" on its line for the next page; unfolded or not is kept per project (`even-keel.outside.archive.<project>`). The view reads both anew, without the loader, after every edit in the task card (`TaskCard`'s `onChanged`, the `edits` count of `Project.tsx`) and after each read of the project's tracker (`TrackerView.readAt`), so a changed status moves the row to its section or into the archive at once. Fake: `/p/ABC?view=outside` (`docs/web/fake-states.md`).
- The parents view: the parents come from the server in `EvenKeelState.parents` (`ParentTask`: a task with `subtasks` and `closedSubtasks`; the open ones of the read, the closed ones the read lists, and those a chain session turned into parents), any status: `parentBlocks` makes them rows of the same `ChainList` — closed first by number for the archive, open by the chain's milestones then number — without a number, a drag, ghosts or the into/out of the chain buttons, and with a chip `closed/all` in the family's rail colour (`familyHue`). A row opens its panel as in the chain, the address keeping the view (`rowSearch`); the panel only reads: transcript and the task's sessions, no chat, Skip or New session, and its transcript window says why the field is shut (`WindowTask.parent`, `t.session.chat.parent`): an answer with a cold cache would offer a new session, which would run an agent on the parent. A press on a family's rail in the chain (`RailLink`, a wider hit strip over the 2 px rail) opens the parent in this view. While the parents are shown, the chain's button has a dot when the chain is paused or its current task waits for a reply (`chainCalls`); the head's chip stays as it is. Each view keeps its own last frame for the chain's motion. Fake: `/p/ABC?view=parents` (`ABC-16`, `ABC-17`, the closed `ABC-21`), with `chain=paused` for the dot.
- Links of a task (`taskLinks` in `web/src/state.ts`, `web/src/ui/Links.tsx`): every chain block and backlog row ends with two counters, a lock for the open tasks it waits for and a key for the open ones waiting for it, 0 included so the column stays in place; a pointed counter lists them in a popover (top layer, so the scrolling panel does not clip it), and a task there opens its card. A live session's metrics (`web/src/ui/BlockMetrics.tsx`, BTN-299, BTN-331), each part with its figures in a `Tipped` tip: the cache as a ring that drains to cold (`cacheShare`; green, amber in its last fifth, a dashed grey ring once cold), the context with its percent (blue, amber, red by the `context` thresholds), the account's initial in its colour only when the session runs on an account other than the project's (`foreignAccount` in `web/src/accounts.ts`: no folder with that e-mail lists the project in `AccountEntry.projects`), and the context resets, hidden at 0. The resets (`contextResets` in `web/src/state.ts`) count every new session with a handoff — checkpoint, limit, another cause of a `relay`, a restart by hand — and every compaction of the task's sessions; the tip breaks them down, and a session's row in the panel's session list says how it was launched and adds its compactions; the list's head is only its name. Compactions come from the transcripts the monitor follows (`TranscriptSummary.compactions`: Claude's `compact_boundary`, Codex's `compacted` line, `docs/unverified.md`), kept per session in memory (`SessionMonitor.compactionsOf`) and put on `TaskSession.compactions` when there are any, so a session gone from the agents' list before the service started shows none. A row whose session opens its second line (`showsAction`: working, and the lead's also on an error or a limit) gives the title the whole first line and puts `ActionMeter` under it, as wide as the title and the side: the current action (`ActionScreen`, an empty prompt while there is none; an API error or a limit is the action until the session acts again, in red with "!") on a field filled to the context level with a hard edge (`--fill`, registered so a new level slides), then the initial, the resets and the percent; it opens with `action-open` as one piece. Any other metered row stays one line with the metrics as a plate (`BlockMetrics`) before the links; a later chip of the row goes on its end. The cache ring takes the number's place when the row is current (instead of the former ▶, kept only while there is no cache) or has no number, and the plate's ring otherwise. A backlog row shows priority bars, the key with the icon button "Into the chain" left of the whole row, the milestone and the counters.
