# The project page: motion and small things

- Motion of changes (`web/src/ui/motion.ts`, `web/src/pages/useChainMotion.ts`): data on the page changes at once; the motion only marks the change and plays only while the page is visible (`visibilityState`). The page keeps the last frame the user saw per project and view — the chain, the parents, each machine's view (`ChainList`'s `machine`) — in memory, across page switches, and on return plays the net change since it: a task that came and went out of sight is not shown. A switch of the view is a filter, not a change of the chain (BTN-1577): the rows swap at once, a motion still playing in the view left ends with it (`Play.view`), and a lead card that mounts in the switch's render (`useSwitched`) shows its action field open and its line whole (`block__now--still`, `screen__line--still`, `still` of `ActionScreen`/`Typed`), taken at mount so a later line still types in. Beats go in turn, one step (`--motion-step`, 0.25 s, `STEP_MS`) apart (BTN-1613): a finished task gets a check, a running one let go takes its own frame back (the part beat, after the check is drawn, `STAMP_MS`), a leaving one folds away in a step (a closed one up into the archive line, whose count bumps; the copy slides under the milestone line, which has the page's background), new ones unfold, the taken one gets its frame drawn around in a step and its lamp and cache ring (or ▶) light up. The taken block also grows into the lead tier only on its own beat (its size, background, title and action field wait for `--beat-take`) and types its action once the frame is drawn (`delay` of `ActionScreen`/`Typed`), so it does not look current before the previous task has closed. A change that comes mid-motion plays alongside it (`stageOf`): running marks and leaving copies live to their own end, a block can carry marks of several motions, each mark with its own delay (`--beat-enter`, `--beat-finish`, `--beat-take`). More than five entries and exits at once — no beats: a line "While you were away: …" for 6 s that unfolds and folds back by height, so the list slides rather than jumps, and a soft glow on the new tasks. The line's counts are full, the tasks under a folded milestone included; the glow is only on the ones in sight. A key prefix gone from the chain while another came is a tracker switch (BTN-1200): its tasks were renamed, not gone and new, so they are in no count and the line, shown whatever the number, says the project moved and names the new keys and the old. A task arriving from the backlog keeps its own flight. With reduced motion nothing moves and no leaving copies are drawn.
- A new order from the server glides (BTN-1612, `useRowGlide`, `reorderShifts` in `web/src/ui/reorder.ts`): another tab's or the phone's drop, a rule, a failed drop sent back, a lane taking a row that joins no group. After each render the rows on screen before and after whose place among themselves changed start where they stood and glide to `translate: none` in `--motion-fast` (0.15 s, `FAST_MS`), at once, with no beat; the rows out of the longest run that kept its order (the one that moved; of two that swap, the one going up) pass over the others. A row that came or went, or only moved because one did, keeps its own motion (enter, leave, the archive fold), so a done task's rows still rise by the fold of its copy. A move into the running group or out of it is the group's step instead (below, 0.25 s), and so is every row it pushes in that render. Nothing glides while a card is dragged and for two settles after its drop (`held` of `useChainDrag`, whose drop has its own glide), in the render that switches the view, or with reduced motion. `?chain=reorder` in `dev:fake` reorders the chain every 2 s.
- The running group "Under way" (BTN-1613, `web/src/ui/runningGroup.ts`, `web/src/pages/useRowGlide.ts`): two running tasks and more — lanes of different machines — share one frame instead of a frame each: the first unbroken run of rows a lane holds in the first section that has one, a question or an error included (a milestone's line between running rows parts them). The frame is `.chain__group`, a layer of the list placed over its rows' rects after each render and each frame of a glide, with the caption "Under way" on its top edge. The caption is a pill (BTN-1715): a 2 px border in the group's colour, as thick as the line, on the page's fill, centred on the line (`top: -1px`: the line runs 1 px above the frame's padding box), its border changing colour with the text. The frame paints the group's fill, its `::before` the line and the halo, and that layer's mask cuts a gap as wide as the caption (`--caption-width`, measured in `place`) out of the top edge, so the halo does not show over the pill's rounded ends (BTN-1624); its edge takes the colour of the state that needs the user most — error (or a limit) over a question over work (`groupTone`, the lamps, not the tracker's question label). Its rows have no frame or fill of their own, a thin line between them (`block--grouped`, `block--group-first|inner`). One running task keeps its own frame and no caption, as before. A row comes in when a lane takes it, from below the locked rows too: on the take beat it glides to its place in a step (FLIP on `translate`, over the rows it passes, those rows gliding with it) holding its own frame (`block--join`; the one row the group forms around, `block--merge`), and in the next step that frame melts into the line. A row its lane lets go (stop after the turn) takes its own frame back on the part beat (`block--part`) and glides to its place in the queue a step later, the frame held around it until then; a done one does the same after its check, then folds into the archive. When one row is left, the shared frame fades and its own comes back in the same step (`block--solo`). `moveDelay` and `regroups` (`motion.ts`) say when each row moves. A drag never drops inside the group or over it (BTN-1611). A fix of main or a carry that runs is a member like any running task; a task that stands at the head — interrupted by the fix, or current on a paused lane (`halted`) — is not, and stands right under the frame with its own (`underWayFirst`, BTN-1617): when main goes red its row parts and the fix's joins, when the fix lands the fix parts for the archive and the task joins again once its session runs (`?chain=fixGroup`). With reduced motion the rows and the frame jump. Back on a view whose group changed out of it (BTN-1672: a machine's view switched to "All machines" after a lane took a task), the net change still marks its rows, but no row glides: the places on record are the view left's, and a row would fly in from where it stood there.
- From 1200 px the project page always keeps the side column, so the chain does not change width when a panel opens; with no panel open it holds `IdlePanel` — an ASCII sailboat (`web/src/ui/voyage.ts`), a meme and a hint. Below 1200 px nothing is reserved and `IdlePanel` is not mounted at all (`useWide`), so its loops do not run unseen; in a hidden tab the boat's interval stops (`useVisible`), the meme's `requestAnimationFrame` is paused by the browser itself. Arriving at a project page with an empty hash opens the session panel of the chain's current task (`focusOnEntry` in `web/src/ui/focus.ts`): the hash is set with `replace`, so "back" still leaves the page, and only once per arrival — a mount or another project key — so the cross keeps the panel closed. The boat is left for a project without a current session. Up to 760 px, where the panel covers the whole window, nothing opens by itself.
- The home page's line about questions uses `questionPath` in `web/src/state.ts`. It links to `/p/<id>#<ID>` for the first open question with a session, in project and chain order. A task that has run or is starting qualifies while it is free to run (`hasSession`). When none qualifies, the link opens `/p/<id>` for the first project with an open question.
- The meme (`web/src/ui/memes.ts`): `momentsOf` reads the page's situation — no open tasks, empty backlog, chain stopped, running, unattended or waiting out a limit, a task on its 3rd session, a checkpoint handoff, over $1000 in the strip's 30 days — and a quarter of the draws take one of these moments' lines (a moment holds as long as the page stands still, so a bigger share made it a refrain) (`t.project.idle.moments`); the rest take a general line (`t.project.idle.memes`), `drive`, the one line that switches the boat to the old picture crawling on wheels over a hill as over land, or a play (`PLAYS`, `t.project.idle.plays`): a story in acts that pile up like a chat's lines (BTN-1171), so the viewer sees one story go on rather than a new meme: all the acts are laid out from the start, each is typed under the earlier ones 1.5 s after the last is typed (`actEnd`), the earlier ones stay above in the muted colour, and 7 s after the last act the whole pile is selected and deleted at once like a chat (`selectionAt`, `CHAT_SELECT`); with reduced motion every act shows at once. `spoons` is plain nonsense and `slip` the tagline turned around by a slip of the boat's tongue; both go back to the draws after their last act. `picture` moves the voyage its own way (`playMotion`): on "Now I have the full picture" the sea and the boat stop as "Full." starts and the sky (clouds and gulls) once "Picture." is typed, two more acts try to fix it with everything still, and the last act runs the whole voyage backwards (ticks go below zero) and stays for good with the whole pile — no line is drawn after it until the panel is mounted again. A chat (`CHATS`, `t.project.idle.chats`) is a dialogue in the look of Claude Code, only on this panel: the human's `>` lines appear whole and muted in mono, the agent's `●` lines are typed, 1.5 s apart (`chatAt`); the lines pile up, all of them laid out from the start so the panel keeps its height, and 7 s after the last one the chat is not backspaced but selected, a line per 0.15 s from the bottom, held selected 0.7 s and deleted at once (`CHAT_SELECT`). Chats are short enough not to need scrolling, so there is none. The `limits` chat (BTN-1510) acts outside the panel: the agent "fixes" the limits and they really leave the header for 10 s. `pickMeme` offers it only with a reset from `limitsFixReset`: the five-hour window of the account the header shows (`useHeaderFiveHour` in `web/src/layout/limitsFix.ts`, picked as `Header` picks it; none in "All machines") spent from 90%, not `limitsOutdated`, its reset still ahead, and no reduced motion, where the chat shows whole and has nothing to take away. Its lines keep their own beats (`limitsFixStarts`): the third starts 2 s after `Update(Limits.tsx)` is typed, and with it the header's limit segments hide (`LimitSegment`, `.limit--gone`, `visibility` so the header keeps its place; the account name, the button, the accounts panel and the limit banners stay); the human asks 8 s later and at 10 s the limits come back with the CLI's English "You've hit your limit, resets 2pm" in both languages, the reset read from the window at the draw. The panel sets the hiding on each frame (`setLimitsGone`) and clears it when unmounted, so a side panel, another project or page and a window under 1200 px bring the limits back at once, and a tab hidden longer than the rest of the 10 s finds them back on its first frame; a window that drops under 90% mid-chat lets it finish. Nothing reaches the server or storage. `dev:fake`: `/p/ABC?limits=full` puts the header's account at 96% (close the opened session panel first). The line is typed in parts like the home page's (1.5 s between parts) and erased at the same pace before the next one, which `pickMeme` never draws equal to the last: a general line and a moment's line go 7 s after they are typed, the drive stays until the set of moments changes, but no less than those 7 s (the moments change as the page loads, too). The line is as wide as the picture and starts at its left edge, so lines of different length do not shift. With reduced motion the line is shown whole and only the event-driven ones change. The picture is set in 7px type and is as wide as the panel: `useColumns` in `IdlePanel` measures the panel on every resize and passes the column count to `voyageFrame`, so the sea and the sky stretch while the boat keeps its size. The sailing boat rides two endless trains of waves with troughs below the calm level; the water's height inside a row is drawn with `'`, `-`, `.` and `_`, so the slopes are smooth instead of row-high steps. The hull is shorter than half the main wave, and under it the water is drawn up to its bottom, so the boat never hangs over a trough between two crests. The hull never tilts — a hull breaks into steps in ASCII; only the rig leans with the waves and the sway, by a third of their slope, and it is sheared rather than turned, so the boom keeps its row.
The backlog flight's horizontal transform and opacity run through Web Animations, separately from the tracking shell (`.flight-track`): React renders cannot stall the horizontal motion. The shell follows the destination during scrolling and layout changes, writing its geometry only when it changes. A task that has entered the chain replaces its ghost immediately; the 250 ms linger is only for a preview abandoned without a move. `Project` memoizes `chainBlocks` by snapshot and project, so local preview and arrival changes do not rebuild the archive. The archive's ordering uses the sorted ready list described in `chain.md`. The browser regression check and motion frames are recorded in `verified.md` (BTN-515).
