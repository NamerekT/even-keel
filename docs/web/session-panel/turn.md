# The chat: the turn line, delivery and the composer

- The turn line of the chat (BTN-183): while the session's lamp is `working`, a line under the feed shows the time since the turn began (`formatElapsed`, ticking every second on the page) and what the session does — "Running: " and the running call's tool line, or "Thinking". It comes with the window's own reads (`Feed.turn` of `GET /api/sessions/<id>/transcript`), not with the state, so a call starting or ending broadcasts no state: a Claude turn begins at the last prompt that is not a meta line (task notifications too), and a call is running from its `tool_use` until its `tool_result`. The line shows no token count: the transcript writes an answer's `output_tokens` only once the answer is done, so a count would stand still while the model thinks and then jump. A Claude turn is mostly "Thinking": a `Read` or a `grep` returns in milliseconds, and the seconds between calls are the model generating. A Codex turn begins at `task_started`, and the rollout writes a call only once it has finished, so Codex always shows "Thinking". In `dev:fake` ABC-5 runs a shell command, the rest think (`?parallel=2`, the chat of DEMO).
- In the turn line (`TurnRow`, BTN-1427) the elapsed time is a grey `.count-badge` pill right after the lamp, before the action, as wide as "59m 59s" (`.transcript__turn-time`), so the action's text starts at one place, never moves on a tick, and a long one is cut with an ellipsis while the pill stays whole; the pill's tip says when the turn began ("The turn has been going since 16:05"). The window shows the line for `turnSession`: a task chat's session (`ChatTarget.session`) or, in a window without one, its own `session` prop — a project chat's (`ProjectChatWindow`), whose window draws its field through `foot`; before BTN-1427 that branch had no turn line at all. During handover the loader places the phase and elapsed time side by side. The desktop task-panel toggle shows its shortcut on a second line in the tooltip. In the job window, each step’s duration sits beside its state, and clone progress shows the stage and available figures in separate cells.
- The delivery phase of the turn line (BTN-188). An answer stops the session and resumes it with the answer (7–10 s), and the transcript still ends in the turn before: from the send until the answer lands, the line is empty (BTN-1362; before it, "Delivering the answer, the session is starting up"), with no tool of the old turn — the clock of the message's delivery mark (below) tells it. One pure function, `turnPhase` (`web/src/pages/turnPhase.ts`), makes the phase from what the window knows: the last reply sent from this window (browser clock), the chain's delivery in progress (`ChainBlock.answering`, the `starting` of a `CurrentTask` that is `answering`) and the journal's last answer to the session (`TaskSession.answeredAt`, also written for a session answered after its chain stopped, `deliverLoose`, and for a tracker comment's answer). The answer has landed once the transcript has a turn begun at or after it, or a prompt or an answer line written after it, or three minutes passed (the hold of `ResumeHolds`); while the chain still delivers it, it has not. The clock runs from the send, and the turn the answer began goes on counting from it — "Thinking" with the same count, no jump back when the prompt lands; a turn begun more than three minutes after the answer counts from its own prompt. Codex is the same: its answer is a new `codex exec resume`, the new turn begins at its `task_started`. In `dev:fake` an answer from the chat stays in the delivery phase for three minutes: the fake transcript gets no new prompt.
- The delivery mark of the user's message (BTN-1362, `DeliveryMark` in `web/src/pages/DeliveryMark.tsx`, `sentFeed` in `web/src/pages/sentLines.ts`), in the task's chat and the project chat alike. Under every user bubble, left of its time, an icon: a muted clock while a sent or queued message is awaiting its transcript line ("Delivering"; its hands turn, the minute one in `--motion-tick`, the hour one twelve times slower, and stand with reduced motion; the send itself — `deliver` of `useLive`, `web/src/live/actions.ts` — stays off the top bar, which a delivery of minutes would leave running, BTN-1393), two green checks once the transcript holds it ("Delivered at HH:MM", the transcript line's time; every user line of the transcript shows them), a red sign when the server refused it ("Not delivered"). The words are the icon's tip (`Tipped`), never a `title`. A refused line stays in the feed without its answer card, while its text and files go back into the draft as before; it counts as no answer to its question, and the next send takes it out. A sent line and the transcript line that lands for it share one React key (`sent-<id>`, one matching transcript reply per send), so the bubble is the same element through the landing however many lines arrive before it: no blink, no jump of the scroll, only the icon changes.
- Banners over the field (BTN-1363, `ComposerBanner` in `web/src/pages/ComposerBanner.tsx`, a `Band` with `chat-banner`). Under the field stands only the status line; everything else the composer says is a banner between the question's panel and the field, a few words at the left, its buttons and ✕ at the right, each in its own `FoldPresence` (`chat__banner`, the question panel's slide). A refused send or accept is `error` (`role="alert"`): its ✕ drops the refusal from the draft (`dismissFailure` of `useChatDraft`, so a reload does not bring it back; an accept's refusal is hidden in the composer) and a refused accept has "Try again". A question the CLI has not saved (`ask.unsaved`) is `warn`, the explanation in the tip of its line, closed per question. "Accept as answer" (`offersAccept`) is `note`: "Close the task with this answer?" and the button, the explanation in the button's tip; its ✕ closes it for the session's current turn, kept in `localStorage` (`acceptClosed.ts`: a session's id → the time of its feed's last line, the latest 200 sessions), so the next turn without a mark shows it again. On a phone the accept banner waits out the keyboard, as the status line. The project chat has the error banner only.
- The menu of the agent's last message (BTN-1363, `MessageMenu` in `web/src/pages/MessageMenu.tsx`, the look of `MoreMenu`'s list): the last text line of the feed opens it on a right click, and on a long press of a finger (500 ms, cancelled by a move of 10 px; Android's own `contextmenu` and the timer for Safari open it once). Its items: "Accept as answer" while `ChatTarget.accepts` (it goes through the composer's handle, so a refusal is the same banner) and "Copy text" where the page has a clipboard. Escape, Tab, a press elsewhere, a scroll and a resize close it. Text selected in the message keeps the browser's menu, and so does a message with no item.
- Mail to a working session (BTN-1490). The send button stays available while the session listens, with its existing hook or steering tip. Each send echoes into the feed immediately. Mailbox rows and CLI queue entries observed in this window share the same delivery marks, including messages from Remote Control. They join the feed only after the window's first read of the transcript: until then a window with a session shows the loader, a reopened project chat's first message included, and does not look starting (BTN-1698, `shownLines` in `web/src/pages/sentLines.ts`); only a message sent from this window, or one with no session to read yet, shows before the read. The clock changes to checks when the matching user line appears in the transcript, including after an inbox delivery or a resume. The bubble and its pictures keep their keys throughout. A returned message shows the red mark with the reason (question, stopped session or closed task). Its text and locally submitted attachments go back ahead of the current draft once. The draft keeps the original files while mail waits and records which returns it has restored. A browser that lacks the original files puts their session paths in the draft.
- The chat and Activity feed follow their end through `useFeedPin` (BTN-1372). A resize observer keeps a pinned chat at its end through new messages, composer changes and image loads. A wheel, touch, scrollbar or keyboard scroll lets the reader move away. A folded message that was never opened does not scroll its toggle into view as it mounts.
- Commands in the chat (BTN-557, `docs/sessions/agents.md`, "CLI and transcripts"). `ChatComposer`'s field is `CommandText` (`web/src/pages/CommandText.tsx`, styles in `chatCommands.css`), modelled on the commands of Claude Desktop. A `/` at the start of the message opens a menu above the field (`menuQuery`, `matchingItems`): ↑/↓ move, Tab or Enter put the command in (`withCommand`), Esc closes, a click only puts it in; the row under the pointer shows its description beside the menu at once, over the menu below 760 px. A known command in the field is drawn in `--accent` by a mirror behind the textarea (the textarea's own text goes transparent while a command is in it, its caret stays), with its arguments hint muted after it until the user types them (`ghostOf`); the pointer resting on it shows a card with the name and the description. The command acts as one token: a caret stepping into it selects it whole (`snappedSelection`), Backspace right after it or Delete right before it removes it whole (`tokenCut`). The pure parts are in `web/src/pages/chatCommands.ts` with their tests; the labels are `commands.menu`, `commands.filter` and `commands.groups` of the composer's i18n.
- The menu's items (BTN-1695). `useChatMenu(project, task, agent)` (`web/src/pages/useChatMenu.ts`) gives `CommandText` a `ChatMenu`: Even Keel's commands (`CHAT_COMMANDS`, expanded by the hub) for a task's session, and for a Claude session the hub's answer to the query `chat/menu` {project, task?} on top — read when the field mounts and again each time the menu opens (`refresh`), so a changed configuration or package set shows without a restart. The hub builds it in `createMenuSource` (`src/sessions/menuSource.ts`) from what the session gets, never asking the session: `chatMenu` (`src/sessions/menu.ts`) reads the user's `commands/*.md` (top level only) and `skills/<folder>/SKILL.md` of the config copy (`configStore` of the global folder), then the same of each plugin folder of the project's packages (`Packages.pluginFolders(project, task)`, read only), named `<plugin>:<name>`. A skill is named by its folder, `user-invocable: false` hides it, the description is its frontmatter's `description` (a small parser of its own, `frontmatterOf`); user entries come before package ones, each part sorted, the first of two equal names wins. A task's session with "Trim the tools" on drops the names its `skillOverrides` turn `off` (`trimSettings`, `src/tools/settings.ts`; `user-invocable-only` stays); a project chat (task null) gets no trim. `menuItems` (`chatCommands.ts`) joins the parts into groups `even-keel`, `user`, `package`, Even Keel's command winning on a name; the list shows a heading per group (`li.chat__commands-group`) only when it has more than one. A user or package item goes to the session as typed (`commandPrompt` and `typedText` leave an unknown `/name` alone) and the agent expands it; other agents than Claude get only Even Keel's commands, since Codex calls a skill as `$name` (`docs/unverified/sessions.md`). A project chat has no `/handoff`: a Claude chat shows only the hub's part, any other agent's gets no menu and no `/` button (`useChatMenu` gives `null`). dev:fake answers `chat/menu` with `FAKE_MENU` (`web/src/live/fakeMenu.ts`).
- How a live transcript window reads. The window reads its session's transcript when the service says the file changed, and every 5 s besides in case a word was missed. Reading registers the file with `TranscriptWatch` (`src/sessions/watch.ts`, the file from the optional `Agent.feedFile`): an `fs.watch` on it, whose events settle for 100 ms into one SSE `transcript` event `{sessionId}` (`EventHub.signal`: `broadcast` drops a frame equal to the last one, and this one always is); a file nobody read for 30 s is dropped, and a watcher that fails is dropped and logged while the poll goes on. Each read folds the whole file again. Checked on 2026-10-18 on a live Claude session on Windows: 31 writes in 4 min, every one followed by an event, the latest 260 ms after. The session cards and the chain keep `SESSION_POLL_MS` (3 s), since each tick starts `claude agents` once per account folder (~260 ms each).
- `POST /api/session/answer` takes `{project, task, sessionId, ask, answers, text, files: [{name, data}]}`: `ask` is the open question's `tool_use` id or `null` for a plain-text question, `answers` maps the question text to the answer (labels joined with `, ` for `multiSelect`, the Other text as is), `data` is base64. The route alone accepts a body up to 64 MB. Refusals are the codes of `Dispatcher.answerable` (`docs/chain.md`).
OpenCode tool-result pictures use the same transcript thumbnails and viewer (BTN-481). Its whole fold emits `image` lines from the count of image attachments stored in `opencode.db`; its `Agent.image` reads the selected base64 attachment by session and call id (`sessionImage`, `docs/sessions/opencode.md`, "OpenCode"). The original file and worktree are not needed. Checked with a saved real `read` result in an isolated transcript window at desktop and mobile sizes.
