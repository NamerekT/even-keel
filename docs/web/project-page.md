# Page: the project page

- The task card (`web/src/pages/TaskCard.tsx`, loaded lazily together with `react-markdown`): a task title in the backlog, in the session panel's head and of a locked blocker in the chain opens it; a press anywhere else on a chain row (all but its links and the meters on the right) opens the session panel, the key's link stretched over the row. An open card is the `?task=<id>` parameter in the address, so "back" on a phone closes it (the project's packages are `?pkg=1`, `&add=<plugin>` with the add dialog over them, `ProjectPackages.tsx`, `docs/packages.md`); links to other tasks inside the card replace the parameter without piling up history. Every open reads the card from the tracker; the last card read for each task stays in the tab's memory (`cardMemory.ts`, BTN-655, keyed by the project's GUID and the task, up to 200 cards, never in `localStorage`), so a card opened again shows at once without the loader while it is re-read, the answer replacing it quietly; a failed re-read shows its error above the remembered card, a "no such task" forgets it. A change made in the card reads it anew and so updates the remembered one. The card is 1040 px wide and stands 32 px from the top, not in the middle (BTN-801): while a task is read it is at its full height (`max-height`, the loader filling the body), and once the content shows, a shorter one glides the bottom edge up to it (`useHeightGlide`, keyed by the shown card's id, `--motion-appear`), so a long task never moves and the head stays put; a linked task glides back to the full height for its read, a remembered one straight between the two heights; with reduced motion and on a full-screen card below 760 px nothing glides. Its styles are `card.css` (dialog, head, body layout), `cardMain.css` (the left column), `cardSide.css` (the panel) and `cardNarrow.css` (reduced motion and narrow screens, imported last). Markdown links leading inside the tracker are text, and those with a task number open its card. HTML inside Markdown is rendered through `rehype-raw` and cleaned by `rehype-sanitize`: Huly writes its tables as HTML. The layout is two columns — the description and comments on the left, a panel on the right (on top below 900 px). The panel (BTN-1097) is three zones split by one line each — the action, the properties, the relations; inside a zone groups stand `--space-16` apart and the parts of a group `--space-8`, and every label is one section label (11 px uppercase, muted, 0.04em): short properties (priority, milestone, parent) have it at the left, full-width groups ("Runs on", labels, attachments, relation groups) above. The action is "Into the chain" or "To the backlog" in a `ButtonWithTip`, what it does behind its "?", a refusal as a warn line under it. The properties start with two facts, "Estimate" (`card.estimate`) and "Time" — the agent's active time over the whole task, the `/stats` "Time" column, sent with the card as `activeS` (`docs/stats.md`); the time takes the warning tone over ×1.5 of the estimate (`cardTime.ts`, the "Within ×1.5" bound of `/stats`), a missing one is "—" and neither hides the row. Relation keys share one column width, the longest key of the card in `ch` (`--ref-key`), so the titles start on one line; where the tracker has `milestones`/`moveMilestone` (`docs/trackers.md`) the milestone is a `Select` that moves the task at once, without a confirmation: the new value shows right away and goes back with the error if the tracker refuses; a question or excluding label is a callout above the description. Under the milestone, "Runs on" (`TaskRunPick.tsx`, BTN-508, BTN-983) reads the task's run from `state.runs` (`runPickOf` in `runPick.ts`), not its labels: a provider `Select` — first "As the project" — for a provider a free model field suggesting the models of the project's account for it (`SuggestField`, `useModelCatalog`), and an effort `Select` with the levels of the provider picked, or of the project's one under "As the project" (`effortsOf`), absent where that provider takes none; a new provider clears the model and an effort it does not take (`withProvider`); the three are fields of one look, height and left edge. It does not save on a pick: Save (or Enter in the model field), shown only when the pick differs from the run at the right of the "Runs on" label so nothing below moves, sends `POST /api/task/run` with the whole run (`runPickChange`; every field empty for no run), which touches no tracker, so the pick works on any adapter. A live session and a pending change get a note: the run takes effect from the task's next session (a record, not a touch of the session). The task's labels are plain tags, a `<Provider> | <model>` one among them. Attachments are tiles in that panel between the facts and the relations (`Attachments.tsx`): only photos and videos open, in `MediaViewer.tsx` over the whole window (arrows, ←/→, swipe, a click on a picture zooms, Esc goes back to the card), any other file downloads. A picture tile is the full file loaded lazily; a video tile has no preview on purpose — without `Range` support even `preload=metadata` could pull the whole video.
- Editing in the card (BTN-771, a task of the built-in tracker, whose card carries `edit`; `CardFields.tsx`, `CardLinks.tsx`, `CardTree.tsx`, `useCardEdit.ts`, the pure rules in `cardEdit.ts`, styles in `cardEdit.css`): the card is where such a task changes, so it has no "Open in …" link and no "Into the chain"/"To the backlog" buttons — the status pick in the head does the same. The title edits in place: Enter, leaving the field, closing the card or hiding the tab saves it, Escape drops it, an empty one is not saved. What was sent stands in the field from the moment it is left until the card is read anew, and a saved description in its view the same way (BTN-1111, `useHeld.ts`); only a refusal brings the old value back. A typed edit is kept in the browser until it lands (BTN-1027, see "Drafts" below). The status is a `Select` of the seven fixed statuses: Backlog → a `ToDo` status is `task/ready`, a `ToDo`/`Active` one → Backlog `task/unready` (off with its reason while a session is live or the chain refuses the task), anything else an `edit`. Priority is a `Select`; the milestone a `SuggestField` creating a new one by its typed name, with a warning under it when the project's "Project tasks" rules leave that milestone out; labels a `SuggestField` of the project's labels and the built-in ones with their meaning, a new name creating the label. Relations: "+ Add a link" opens the kind (`blocks`, `blocked by`, `relates to`, focused first) and a task search over every built-in project (`builtin/search`); a refusal stands under the search, × on a row removes the link. A subtask shows the path of its ancestors over the title (each opens its card) and a Parent field with ×; "Change the parent" (or "Pick a parent") searches the task's own project. A parent has "Subtasks · closed/all" under the description in the chain's order and "+ Add subtask" (none on a closed task): the subtask takes the parent's milestone and Todo; the first one of a task in work asks first, since the task leaves the chain while its session goes on. A parent's milestone change asks whether its open subtasks move too (yes by default). "Delete" in the card's ⋯ menu asks, listing the subtasks that become top-level; it is off while the task is current or has a live session. Every field edit sends what the field held and the new value; a `builtin.conflict` opens "The task changed while you were editing it" with theirs and yours, "Keep mine" re-sending it over theirs; a refusal of any other kind is a band in the card. Escape in a dialog opened from the card closes only that dialog (`ConfirmDialog` stops `cancel`, which React passes up its tree). A subtask's rail in the chain has the parent's key and title as its tip (`t.chain.family`), a press on it opening the parent in the "Parents" view, and a backlog row of a subtask shows `↳ <parent key>`. Lists opened from the card are at most 420 px wide, a long title ending in "…". The refusals are in `docs/trackers.md` ("The built-in tracker"); the fake is `?card=builtin|conflict` (`docs/web/fake-states.md`).
- The journal in the card (BTN-774, `CardJournal.tsx`, the pure rules in `journalView.ts`, styles in `cardMain.css`): every tracker's comments go oldest first, the last 20 shown and "N more" above them showing the rest. A built-in task's section is "Journal · N", its entries named "You" (the person's notes, framed in the accent), "Agent · <model>" (the model once the comment carries it) or "Even Keel" with its mark; another tracker's comment keeps its author. The replies in a comment's thread (BTN-909, `TaskComment.replies`, only Huly gives them) stand under its bubble, indented by the avatar's column with a line at their left, oldest first (`repliesOf`), never folded and not counted in the heading, with a smaller avatar and the same author and time; the chat's task panel shows them too. Only for a built-in task: a trash button on the person's own note asks and sends `builtin/uncomment`; an empty journal says what will appear in it; the note field at the bottom sends `builtin/comment` on Enter (Shift+Enter a new line; on a touch screen Enter is a new line and the send button sends), with a line under it that a note does not reach the session — its questions are answered in the session's chat. Closing the card asks nothing: an unsent note stays as the field's draft and is back in it on the next open, until it is sent or the field emptied. The fake is `?card=builtin&journal=empty|long|asked` (`docs/web/fake-states.md`).
- The description editor (BTN-772, a built-in task's card; `CardDescription.tsx` in the card's chunk, `DescriptionEditor.tsx` in its own lazy chunk with TipTap — ~519 kB, ~163 kB gzip, loaded only when a description is edited; the pure Markdown side in `descriptionDoc.ts`, `descriptionLoss.ts`, `descriptionPaste.ts`; styles in `description.css`): "Edit" by the heading (its tip says a double click on the text opens it too) turns the description into the editor; the text is always Markdown, and the Preview/Markdown switch (a `Segmented`, remembered in `even-keel.description.look`) only changes how it is edited. Preview is TipTap with a toolbar (bold, italic, strike, code, H2, H3, lists, a checklist, a quote, a code block, a table, a link typed in a field of its own); it writes the text only on a real edit, so opening and closing it changes nothing, and an edit undone back is no change. The visual editor is off, with the reason as the switch's tip, when it would lose a part of the text: HTML, a footnote, a picture or anything it would write back otherwise (`previewLoss`, which compares the text with its round trip through the editor's schema); a table is written unpadded (`compactTable`). Markdown is a plain textarea; HTML pasted into it becomes Markdown (`htmlToMarkdown`). A file pasted (Ctrl+V) or dropped into either view becomes the task's attachment (BTN-773, `useUploads.ts`, the pure side in `descriptionUpload.ts`): one over the "Attachment size limit, MB" setting is refused before the upload with the `builtin.fileTooLarge` band and its button to Settings → Interface; otherwise an "uploading…" mark goes in at the cursor and is replaced by `![name](url)` for a raster picture or `[name](url)` for anything else once the upload answers. The visual editor has no picture node, so a file in Preview switches to Markdown (not remembered) and the mark goes at the end. A drag over the editor shows the drop overlay; Save is off, with the hint saying so, while an upload pends. Ctrl+Enter saves in both (before ProseMirror's own Mod-Enter), Escape leaves, asking first when there are changes, and Escape in that question closes only the question. Saving sends `builtin/edit` with `field: 'description'`, the text the editor started from and the new one, and nothing when they are equal. A `builtin.conflict` opens "The description changed while you were editing it": the new version against yours line by line (`DiffLines`, shared with the changelog), the count of the lines keeping yours would overwrite, "Keep mine" re-sending yours over theirs and "Take the new one" reopening the editor on theirs, with yours kept under it ("Your version": "Copy", "Put mine back") until the card closes. Closing the card (×, Escape, the backdrop) or the tab with the editor open saves the changes; hiding the tab saves them and keeps the editor open; only the editor's Cancel (and Escape inside it) drops them. The fake is `?card=builtin&description=html|long|empty` and `?card=conflict` (`docs/web/fake-states.md`).
- Drafts (BTN-1027, `cardDrafts.ts`, `useLeaving.ts`): what the person typed into a built-in task's title, description or note is kept in `localStorage` under `even-keel.draft.<project GUID>.<task>.<field>` as `{ from, to }` from the moment it is sent until the service takes it; any refusal keeps it. `useLeaving` saves on `visibilitychange` → hidden, `pagehide` and unmount; a save sent as the page goes away is a `keepalive` request when its body fits 60 000 bytes (`fitsKeepalive`, the browser caps all in flight at 64 KiB), a plain one otherwise, which may not arrive. On the next open of the card a draft whose `to` the field does not hold yet is sent again over its own `from` (once per mount: React's strict mode runs a mount effect twice), so a field changed meanwhile opens the same conflict window; "Take the new one", Escape on the title and Cancel in the editor drop the draft.
- The card's attachments (`Attachments.tsx`, styles in `media.css`): every tracker's files are tiles — a picture, a video with its play mark, anything else its extension — each with its name (cut with "…", whole in the tip) and size under it. Only a built-in task's tile (BTN-773, the card carries `edit` and the file its `id`) has a trash button on its corner, shown on hover or focus and always on a touch screen; it asks "Delete the attachment?" (a link to it in the description stops opening) and sends `builtin/detach`. A record whose file is gone from the service's disk is a "No file" tile with the reason as its tip, still deletable. The fake is `?card=builtin&files=missing`.
- Adding tasks (BTN-770, a project a tracker view of kind `builtin` lists; `AddGhost.tsx`, `addGhost.css`, the pure rules in `web/src/ui/addGhost.ts`, the project's milestones from its vocabulary in `useProjectMilestones.ts`): a dashed, faded "+ Add task to the milestone <name>" (in the group with no milestone "+ Add task") ends every chain group that shows its rows — not a folded one, not the archive, not a group the "Project tasks" rules shut out (`takesGhost`: a milestone `ownsMilestone` accepts, tasks with no milestone only when the rules let them in). A task made there is Todo with the group's milestone, so it enters the chain at once; while the chain runs, the ghost's tip says it is taken in its turn. A milestone with no task yet (one of the vocabulary's milestones no task carries, `milestoneView`'s `empty`) is an empty group with only its ghost, placed by the vocabulary's order; a project with no task at all has one "+ Add task" as its whole empty state (`looseGhost`). The "Backlog · N" tab of such a project stands at 0 too, and the panel has "+ Add task" on top: Backlog, no milestone, the new row unfolding under the ghost (`backlog__row--new`). A press (mouse, Enter, Space) turns the ghost into a title field of its height in the same place. Enter sends `builtin/create` (`project`, `title`, `status`, `milestone`) and keeps the field open and empty for the next one; Ctrl+Enter also opens the new task's card; Escape closes it, keeping its text as that ghost's draft (`draftKey`, in memory until the page reloads, never shared between tabs); leaving it closes only an empty one. A blank title creates nothing and says so under the field; a refusal stays there as its code's text with the title kept; a second Enter before the answer sends nothing. The fake is `?tracker=builtin` (`docs/web/fake-states.md`).
- The changelog of a milestone (BTN-142, BTN-209, `web/src/pages/ChangelogDialog.tsx`): a milestone head of the chain — not "no milestone", not the archive, not of a project without git (BTN-963: no branch to commit the file to, and its tasks change no code) — ends with a sheet-and-pen button (its tip says what it is) and, once the milestone's section has been written to the file, "+N since written": the done tasks of the milestone in `state.tasks` that the last written version does not account for (`state.changelogs`, counted by the page in `web/src/changelog.ts`). The button opens a modal that asks `changelog/open` at once, which never runs the agent (BTN-747): the newest version kept in the database comes back right away; without one, `ChangelogView.version` is `null` and `start` holds the milestone's done and open task counts and the section the file has now. Then the modal shows the counts, the file's section as it is (or "no draft yet") and only the primary "Write draft" (`changelog/draft`, the agent's first draft; a second `draft` returns the version it finds), greyed through `aria-disabled` with its reason at the left when no task is done; the edit box, "Add N", "Write again", "Copy", "Write" and the cost come with the first version. While the agent works — a first draft, "Write again", "Add N", an edit — the body is the page's loader (`Loading`, `fit="fill"`) with what it does under the boat; the window keeps one size whatever it holds (760 × 880 px at most), the text box taking the room left and scrolling, so it never jumps between the loader, a short draft and a long one. Above the text box, under the counts: the version's number and time, or the file and the short commit it was written by ("already in" when the file had the text), and at its right the "Preview / Markdown" switch (`Segmented`, kept in `localStorage` `even-keel.changelog.look` for every milestone, "Preview" by default). "Preview" renders the section through `SessionMarkdown` with no marks; "Markdown" shows it line by line against the version's `base` (`diffSection`, `src/changelog/diff.ts`): added lines green, removed ones struck through on red, a line with a few words replaced shows those words; the first version is compared with the file's section, so the user's own edits are not marked as the agent's. Also above it — how many done tasks the version accounts for and how many are open, the user's words of an edit, the tag a released milestone has. Under it — a box "what to change": Enter sends `changelog/edit` with the words, Shift+Enter is a new line. The foot's buttons never wrap or shrink; at its left, cut with an ellipsis when short of room, what the version's turn cost (its tokens when the agent counts no cost) and, past one turn, "… in all" for every version of the milestone (`ChangelogView.spent`); pointed at, the account folder, the model, the time, the tokens by kind, whether it came out of the account's limits or its API key's credit, and that `/stats` does not count these turns; "Add N" (`changelog/add`, only when N done tasks are not in the version); "Write again" (`changelog/again`); "Copy" (the clipboard API, else a hidden field inside the dialog and `execCommand('copy')` — plain HTTP from another host has no clipboard API); "Write" (`changelog/write`, primary, greyed once written). The server side and the freeze of a released section are in `docs/releases.md`. An error stays in the modal (`changelog.*` codes). Where the tracker lacks `milestoneTasks` or the agent lacks `write` the button still shows and the modal says so (`docs/sessions/chat.md`, "One-off turns").
- The project head (`web/src/pages/Project.tsx`, `project.css`): the key, the display name, the questions chip and the tracker read on the left. The tracker read (`TrackerRead`) is a refresh icon that reads the project's tasks now (`POST /api/chain/refresh` with `{project}`; the icon spins while it runs); "updated N ago" from `trackers[].readAt[<key>]`, the time of the project's last successful read, is the button's tip and stands beside it, amber, only while the tracker is down or the project was not read for over 3 minutes (`readLate` in `web/src/pages/status.ts`, BTN-1042); the poll is every 30 s, the button is for a change made in the tracker a moment ago. The name is the config's `projects[].name` (`projectName` in `web/src/state.ts`); `ProjectName.tsx` edits it in place — a click turns it into an input, Enter or leaving it saves through `project/settings`, Escape cancels. A project without a name shows "Add a name" only while the key is pointed at or the button has the keyboard's focus, folded to nothing otherwise; a touch screen (`hover: none`) never shows it, and the name is added in the settings' "Repository" tab, where the same editor is a field ("Project name", saved at once, outside the tab's draft). Everywhere else a project is named by `web/src/ui/ProjectLabel.tsx` — the key, then the name when there is one: the header's crumb, the account menu, the queue panel, the home card, the launch chart's legend. The chain's status and the two buttons that change it are one block of segments at any width (`.hblock.project__buttons`, BTN-1042): the status first, a segment tinted as its chip, then the buttons, the primary one filled; why a button is off is that button's `title`, not a line. Before the block stands the "What sessions get" chip (BTN-615, `ToolsChip` in `web/src/pages/ToolsLink.tsx`, once a full-width band under the strip): amber with the count while the project's settings have problems or the chain waits for the tracker's package, quiet while only the tracker's plugin is advised, absent when everything reaches the sessions or the settings are still being read; its tip holds the count and the tracker's line, a press goes where the "Open →" of the band went (`docs/packages.md`). The CI badge, "Backlog · N", "Settings" and the help "?" are tabs over the side column (`.side-tabs`), one strip with dividers: CI and "?" narrow ones, the other two share the rest; the strip is flat at the bottom and stands on the card under it, whose top corners are square; each opens its own card (`#ci`, `#backlog`, `#settings`, `#help`), and the open one takes the card's background. From 761 px the card's frame does not animate, only its content fades in (`fade-in`); up to 760 px the panel still slides in. With no side column (under 1200 px with no panel open) they end the head row as the chip and the tags. Up to 760 px the head is built from the header's blocks (`.hblock`, the `look` of `chipLook.ts`): they are one block of segments at the end of the title row — CI with its dot, the backlog's icon and count, the settings' icon, the "?" — and the next row ends with the chain's block.
- The project page's Legend (the "?" tab, `web/src/pages/HelpPanel.tsx`, samples in `HelpSamples.tsx`) groups marks by location: top header, project row, side panel tabs, task card and session strip. QueueContents, WaitingContents and AccountName are shared with the actual header, using the current chain count, names and account colours. QuestionsChip, CacheRing, priority numbers and the remaining icons use their page components and styles. Task state lamps are separate from priority numbers; the lock counters (blockers), key counters (dependents) and lock replacing a lamp (blocker outside the chain) have separate explanations. The session action/context field is distinct from the thin estimate line at the card edge. All state lamps and priorities are explained even on an empty chain; rework, family rails and estimates appear when the chain has them. The panel leaves the chain visible for comparison. Samples are inert; Escape closes the panel.
- Folding on the project page (`web/src/pages/ChainList.tsx`): the divider line itself is the button. A hidden milestone is a dashed line that reveals it; the header of a revealed one folds it together with those after it. The archive is one line above the chain: folded, a dashed line with the count that opens the latest page; open, a block of its own (`archiveView` gives its pages apart from the chain, `archived`) — the line "N of M" with "K more" for the next, older page and a press on the line folding it all, and under it the closed tasks as quiet one-line rows (`ArchivedRow`: lamp, key, title, the context resets as on a chain row (`ResetsBadge`), the milestone only where it changes) in the columns of the chain's rows, never inside the milestone sections. The last closed task stays a row of the chain. Milestones up to the first one with open tasks cannot be folded (`fixed` in `web/src/ui/milestones.ts`); a chain without milestones has no lines at all, except the archive's.
- A backlog task on its way into the chain (`web/src/pages/useArrival.ts`): while its "Into the chain" button is pointed at (for 250 ms, so a pointer passing over the buttons does not shake the chain) or pressed, a ghost block stands where it would land (`ProjectOrder.landing` from the service, `landingIndex` puts it ahead of the locked blockers of the task it precedes) and the page scrolls to it, revealing a folded milestone; the ghost, in the size of the tier it will take, and a milestone it unfolds grow open and fold shut by height instead of appearing at once. On the press the backlog row slides straight left out of the panel, fading, and folds (`.backlog__row--leaving`; the panel keeps a copy of it until then, should the chain take the task sooner); once it has gone and the task is in the chain, a copy enters at the height of its block from behind the side column (`.flight` stands under `.project__side`) and slides straight left onto it, fading in, so it does not pop up out of nowhere when the column is closed or another card is opened meanwhile (`Flight.tsx`); then the block is marked for a moment. On a narrow screen (≤760 px) and with reduced motion there is no flight: the narrow screen closes the panel instead.
- Into the chain and back (BTN-509, `docs/chain/entry.md`, "Entry into the chain"): "Into the chain" (`readyButton` in `web/src/state.ts`) stands for a backlog task in `UnStarted` where the tracker has `ready`, and for one that lacks a required label (`ProjectOrder.requiredLabel` is `false`) where it has `relabel` — so a GitHub or GitLab issue without the label gets it too; it is off with the reason as its title when the task is in `ProjectOrder.refused`. "To the backlog" (`canUnready`) stands for a chain task in `ToDo` or `Active` where the tracker has `unready`, or one carrying a required label where it has `relabel`, and never while a session of the task is live (`hasLiveSession`): in the card under the facts, and in the chain as `.block__unready` (an arrow out of a box, `OutOfChainIcon`) right before the row's link counters, shown on hover or focus within the row and always on a device without hover.
- The backlog's order (BTN-934, `web/src/pages/backlogOrder.ts`): three segments in the panel's head, by the title — the newest on top (the default), the oldest on top, and by priority (urgent to none, the newest first within one). A task's number stands for its age: the model has no creation date and numbers only grow on every tracker. The page sorts; the service still sends the backlog by number (`byNumber`). The choice is kept in the browser (`even-keel.backlog.order`), one for every project, as Home's grouping. The built-in tracker's add ghost stays first in any order; a new task unfolds at its sorted place, and a row leaving for the chain slides out from its place in the sorted list.
- The backlog's search, pages and runs (BTN-982, `web/src/pages/backlogSearch.ts`): a row's meta line shows the task's run from `state.runs` (the card's "Runs on", BTN-983) by `RunLabelTag` right after its key and parent; the run and the milestone grow from a small least up to their text (the run far faster, the milestone's name giving way first), and a row whose least does not fit wraps its work chip and links (`.backlog__tail`) to a second line instead of cutting the run. A field under the hint filters on the page what is already there: case aside, each word of the query must be somewhere in the key, the title, the run's text (`taskRunText`) or the milestone's name; the order stays the sort's, the head adds "found X of N", nothing found is one muted line. Escape with a query clears it, without one closes the panel; the query is not kept, a closed panel forgets it. The list shows the first `BACKLOG_PAGE` (50) rows after the search and the sort and a "N more" button that adds 50; a change of the query goes back to the first 50. The add ghost stays first, and a row leaving for the chain slides out of the rows shown. `dev:fake` with `?backlog=many` adds 64 backlog tasks to ABC, every fifth with a run.
- A backlog task whose outsider author changed its text after the entry label (`ProjectOrder.changed`, BTN-1124/BTN-1135, `docs/chain/entry.md`) shows an amber "text changed" chip after its key and parent; its tip says to read the task and press "Into the chain", which puts the label on again. `dev:fake` with `?backlog=changed` marks ABC-19.
- The lamp of a task (`taskLamp` in `web/src/state.ts`) is one for every place the task shows — the chain block, the home card, the queue, the blockers list: the tracker status first (done, canceled), then the session; with no session a task in the backlog (status category `UnStarted`) is `backlog` (dotted ring), not `ahead`, so a blocker the chain does not show reads as waiting in the backlog. A finished session of an open task is `stopped` (amber ring with a stop square), not done; a session whose last line is `CHECKPOINT: …` (`SessionView.checkpoint`) is `handoff` (blue arrow) whether its process lives or not, and it does not count as waiting for a reply. A background job that `claude agents` still calls `working` but has no live process 5 min after its launch is finished (`lampOf` in `src/sessions/agents.ts`). Working has a triangle, waiting a question mark, and a completed branch fix a cross, so the marks differ without relying on colour (`ui/Lamp.tsx`, `ui/lamp.css`). Fix and release-carry rows also show the same state lamp beside their key, including in the archive; their Forge smiths identify the service agent, with crossed hammers on a completed fix.

- Motion of changes (`web/src/ui/motion.ts`, `web/src/pages/useChainMotion.ts`): data on the page changes at once; the motion only marks the change and plays only while the page is visible (`visibilityState`). The page keeps the last frame the user saw per project (in memory, across page switches) and on return plays the net change since it: a task that came and went out of sight is not shown. Beats go in turn, 0.4 s apart: a finished task gets a check, a leaving one folds away (a closed one up into the archive line, whose count bumps; the copy slides under the milestone line, which has the page's background), new ones unfold, the taken one gets its frame drawn around and its lamp and cache ring (or ▶) light up. The taken block also grows into the lead tier only on its own beat (its size, background, title and action field wait for `--beat-take`) and types its action once the frame is drawn (`delay` of `ActionScreen`/`Typed`), so it does not look current before the previous task has closed. A change that comes mid-motion plays alongside it (`stageOf`): running marks and leaving copies live to their own end, a block can carry marks of several motions, each mark with its own delay (`--beat-enter`, `--beat-finish`, `--beat-take`). More than five entries and exits at once — no beats: a line "While you were away: …" for 6 s that unfolds and folds back by height, so the list slides rather than jumps, and a soft glow on the new tasks. A task arriving from the backlog keeps its own flight. With reduced motion nothing moves and no leaving copies are drawn.

- The meme (`web/src/ui/memes.ts`): `momentsOf` reads the page's situation — no open tasks, empty backlog, chain stopped, running, unattended or waiting out a limit, a task on its 3rd session, a checkpoint handoff, over $1000 in the strip's 30 days — and a quarter of the draws take one of these moments' lines (a moment holds as long as the page stands still, so a bigger share made it a refrain) (`t.project.idle.moments`); the rest take a general line (`t.project.idle.memes`), `drive`, the one line that switches the boat to the old picture crawling on wheels over a hill as over land, or a play (`PLAYS`, `t.project.idle.plays`): a story in acts, each typed, held 7 s and erased like a general line before the next. `spoons` is plain nonsense and `slip` the tagline turned around by a slip of the boat's tongue; both go back to the draws after their last act. `picture` moves the voyage its own way (`playMotion`): on "Now I have the full picture" the sea and the boat stop as "Full." starts and the sky (clouds and gulls) once "Picture." is typed, two more acts try to fix it with everything still, and the last act runs the whole voyage backwards (ticks go below zero) and stays for good — no line is drawn after it until the panel is mounted again. A chat (`CHATS`, `t.project.idle.chats`) is a dialogue in the look of Claude Code, only on this panel: the human's `>` lines appear whole and muted in mono, the agent's `●` lines are typed, 1.5 s apart (`chatAt`); the lines pile up, all of them laid out from the start so the panel keeps its height, and 7 s after the last one the chat is not backspaced but selected, a line per 0.15 s from the bottom, held selected 0.7 s and deleted at once (`CHAT_SELECT`). Chats are short enough not to need scrolling, so there is none. The line is typed in parts like the home page's (1.5 s between parts) and erased at the same pace before the next one, which `pickMeme` never draws equal to the last: a general line and a moment's line go 7 s after they are typed, the drive stays until the set of moments changes, but no less than those 7 s (the moments change as the page loads, too). The line is as wide as the picture and starts at its left edge, so lines of different length do not shift. With reduced motion the line is shown whole and only the event-driven ones change. The picture is set in 7px type and is as wide as the panel: `useColumns` in `IdlePanel` measures the panel on every resize and passes the column count to `voyageFrame`, so the sea and the sky stretch while the boat keeps its size. The sailing boat rides two endless trains of waves with troughs below the calm level; the water's height inside a row is drawn with `'`, `-`, `.` and `_`, so the slopes are smooth instead of row-high steps. The hull is shorter than half the main wave, and under it the water is drawn up to its bottom, so the boat never hangs over a trough between two crests. The hull never tilts — a hull breaks into steps in ASCII; only the rig leans with the waves and the sway, by a third of their slope, and it is sheared rather than turned, so the boom keeps its row.
