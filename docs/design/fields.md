# Design: fields

The controls of a form and what stands under them. The index, the settings form's layout ("Settings form") and the check of a screen are [`docs/design.md`](../design.md); the values are in [`tokens.md`](tokens.md), the other blocks and the tips in [`blocks.md`](blocks.md). How both settings pages behave is `docs/web/settings-forms.md`.

## Controls

- **Setting** — its name with the "?" and the mark of a change, its control under the name (`Field`, `web/src/pages/settingsParts.tsx`); see "Settings form" in [`docs/design.md`](../design.md#layout).
- **Switch** (BTN-1101) — one option that is on or off is `Switch` (`SwitchField` in a settings form), wherever it stands: a setting, a step of a wizard, a dialog, a filter in a toolbar (wrapped with its words in a `<label>`). A checkbox only picks several items of a list (the kinds of CI, a package's skills, the projects of `/stats`), and a choice of one of several is a radio. The guard refuses a `type="checkbox"` outside a `.map(` over a list ([`tokens.md`](tokens.md#guards)).
- **Segmented choice** (BTN-1090) — one of two to four short options side by side is `Segmented` (`web/src/stats/common.tsx`) or a `.segmented` group (`stats/overview.css`): the wizard's "How tasks work" and "Where a task works", the home grouping, the feed switch, the backlog order, the `/stats` switches, the theme (`ThemeSwitch`, BTN-1089). An item may be an icon and a word, or an icon alone where the row is short (the theme in the gear menu): then its tip is its name and its `aria-label`. The track parts from the page and from a white card in both themes, its edge drawn inside so its size stays; the picked item is `--surface` with `--fg` and a `--border` ring, the rest `--fg-muted`, and a pointer over one of them gives it a light `--fg` tint and `--fg` text (the mixes are in `.segmented`). A page tunes only the items' padding, never the track's colours.
- **Provider choice** — the Agent section of project settings → Sessions and of shared settings → Providers and models (`ProviderChoices`, `docs/web/settings-forms.md`, "Choosing a provider"): a block per provider, an inner block on `--surface-muted`, `--border`, `--radius-control`; the selected one has an accent border, an 8% accent mix into `--surface`, an accent title and a filled corner check. The corner is a radio, and a press on the block selects too, except on its own controls. The fields of the unselected blocks stay editable, since a task's run can pick those providers. The Provider caption holds the help and the mark that the project inherits the provider from the shared settings; the fields stack on a narrow screen.
- **Text area** (BTN-1640) — every `textarea.settings__text` grows with its text, and while empty with its placeholder (`field-sizing: content`), from its `rows` up to seven lines, then scrolls; the handle stays. **How:** `field-sizing` drops `rows`, so `settings.css` gives it back as a `min-height` through `--rows`, set by a rule per `rows` value (1 to 5); a text area with `rows` above 5 needs a new such rule there. A browser without `field-sizing` keeps the `rows` height.
- **Secret field** (BTN-633) — a key, a token or a password is an `<input {...secretInput()}>` (`web/src/ui/secret.ts`), never `type="password"` by hand: a text field dotted by `-webkit-text-security`, so Chrome and Edge do not offer to save it as a password, and a password field only where the browser cannot draw the dots. A form whose fields are secret only some of the time passes the condition, `secretInput(field.secret)`.
- **Suggest field** (BTN-583) — a free text field with suggestions is `SuggestField` (`web/src/ui/SuggestField.tsx`, `suggest.css`, the list drawn by `select.css` and placed and portalled as `Select`'s), never a native `<datalist>`. Focus, by a click or Tab, selects the whole value and opens the list with every option, the current value marked, so the next keystroke replaces it. An option may carry a muted label after its value (a tracker project's key and name).
  - **Keys:** typing filters by value and label (`offersOf` in `selectKeys.ts`); arrows move inside the list without scrolling the page, Enter takes the marked option (or goes to the caller's `onKeyDown` when none is), Escape closes only the list, Tab and blur close it.
  - **Values read in the background** (`loading`, `Select` too, BTN-1257): nothing shows until the field is pressed; pressed while they are read, the list opens at once as `Loader fit="menu"` ("Reading the values…", `aria-busy`), Enter and arrows wait, and when they come the window glides to the list's size (`useListGlide` in `listReading.ts`, `select__list--glide`) — or closes if none came.

## Under a field

- **An explanation is a "?", not a line under the field** (BTN-1590). What a field, a switch or a group is for, what it is kept for, where it can be changed later — goes into the `HelpTip` of its heading, word for word or shorter, as `Row`, `FieldHead` and the dialog `Field` (`AddPackage.tsx`) already do; in a dialog the head is `.add-package__label-row`, the label and its "?". Fields then stand close and the eye sees the fields and the button ("Add a machine", the project wizard). An empty field's prompt is its placeholder.
  - On view under a field stays only its state — the field strip below (an error, "Signed in as …", what a check found); and, as an exception, one short warning line where without it something that cannot be undone is easy to break (`NOTE_EXCEPTIONS`).
  - The note guard counts the lines under fields ([`tokens.md`](tokens.md#guards)). A task that clears an area moves its explanations into the "?" and its states into strips, and lowers the counts.
  - A line that is not under a field stays on view, and its class names what it is, not `note` or `hint` (BTN-1596): a choice card's one-line description is `*__desc`, an option's aside in a menu or a list `*__aside`, a wizard step's, a tile's or a row's state `*__state` (a problem with a button beside it `*__problem`), a list's empty state `*__empty` with its own button.
- **Field strip** — what a field's value was found as, or what is wrong with it, is `FieldStrip` (`web/src/ui/FieldStrip.tsx`, `fieldStrip.css`): a line glued under the field, not muted text below it.
  - **Its look:** as wide as the field, its top tucked under the field's rounded bottom (the field keeps its corners), 22 px of `--surface-muted` mixed 10% toward `--fg` (so it parts from a card and from a `--surface-muted` box alike), `--fg` 12 px text, `--radius-strip` corners, sliding out from under the field (`appear`). An error is the same strip on `--error-bg`/`--error-fg`, the field's border takes its red, and it wraps to as many lines as it needs.
  - **Its room** is kept while it is empty, so a line that comes later moves nothing; a field that only ever shows an error folds it (`fold`): no room until the error, then the strip grows out. A text that does not fit drops to its short form (`StripLine.short`: the login alone for "signed in as …"), then is cut with an ellipsis and the whole of it in a tip (`stripFit`).
  - **Where it stands:** the tracker block (`TrackerFields`: the profile's sign-in, the tracker project's name, its errors and the wizard's check); a settings field refused on save (`ErrorStrip`, `Row`'s `error`; `wide` for a field that fills its line), where the row's name then stands at the field's text (`baseline`), not at the middle of field and strip; and a single field's error anywhere else — the key of a new API account and of a replaced one, the wizard's own key, the page token, a dialog `Field`'s `error` (`AddPackage.tsx`, whose `line` also carries the profile dialog's passed check, "Signed in as …", under its last sign-in field). The chain's state on the project page hangs under the chain's buttons as the same strip ([`docs/web/project-page/head.md`](../web/project-page/head.md), "The run strip").
  - **A setting of several controls** — a switch, a table, a group of numbers, a list — keeps its error as a red line under the whole of it (`FieldNote`), since no one field holds it. It is a state, not an explanation, and its class is `settings__error`, so the note guard does not count it.
- **The error of a whole form** — a failed sign-in, a profile the tracker refused, blank fields named together — stays a band (`notice notice--error`). In a dialog that band stands between the scrolling body and the foot (`add-package__failure`, `role="alert"`), never inside the body, where a long form on a low screen keeps it below the fold (BTN-1031; the guard refuses a `notice--error` inside an `add-package__body`). It is rounded and inset by the body's 20 px from the dialog's sides and 12 px above the foot's line: a rounded block flush with a border or a line reads as cut off.
