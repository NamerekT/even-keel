# Design: blocks

The shared blocks of the page and how each looks. The index and the check of a screen are [`docs/design.md`](../design.md); the values are in [`tokens.md`](tokens.md), the fields of a form in [`fields.md`](fields.md).

Take the component that exists — the shared ones are in `web/src/ui/blocks.tsx`, styled by `web/src/ui/blocks.css` (`ui-*` classes) — rather than inventing a variant. A page adds a class of its own for what is its own (`className`), it does not redraw the block. Where an item has two parts, the first is the rule every screen follows, the second (**How**) is what one needs who changes the component.

## Cards and facts

- **Card** — `Card`: `--surface`, 1 px `--border`, `--radius`, padding 14–16 px, a column with 12 px gaps. Its title is 16 px 600 on a line of its own, actions at the right of the same line. Every group of content on a page is a card; a group is never only a caption between two lines. A page whose section already draws the box uses `CardHead` alone (`Section` of `/stats`).
- **Section label** — `SectionLabel`: 11 px uppercase muted, to split one card into parts. Never the title of a card and never a whole page's structure.
- **Tile** — `Tile` in a `TileGrid`: one number and what it is: label 13 px muted, value 24 px 600 tabular, a note 13 px muted; the grid is `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(170px, 1fr))`.
- **Fact** — `Fact` in a `FactList`, for two to six facts about one thing; a definition list in plain text is not used. A small box on `--surface-muted`, `--radius-control`: an 11 px uppercase label over a 14 px value, `tone` `warn` or `danger` for a value that needs the eye, `wide` across the list. A fact holds its figure alone, with no line under it: the details (when a session started, how long its cache lives) are a tip on the figure (`TipValue`), so a row of facts stays one height at any width. Two short facts may share a row with the label and the value on one line (`panel__inline`: Model over two columns, its name cut with an ellipsis, Tokens over one). A fact whose value is a bar is one line too, split as a segmented box (below): the label, a full-height divider, the bar, a short divider, the figures (`.panel__context`, the context of a session).
- **Nested group** (BTN-576) — a card whose content splits into groups of rows with heads of their own (the accounts page: a card per agent, a group per connection) puts each group into an inner box: `--surface-muted`, 1 px `--border`, `--radius-control`, 12 px apart (the card's gap). Its head is the group's name in 14 px 600, a muted count and the column names in 11 px uppercase, the rows under it split by 1 px `--border` lines; the head is there even when the card holds one group. Things that differ inside one group (windows a plan has) are named in the row, not in the shared head. A group that belongs to someone — who bills an account's connection — takes a 3 px stripe on its left edge as `box-shadow: inset 3px 0 0 <colour>`, so the rounded corners stay whole, the colour one of `--owner-*` ([`tokens.md`](tokens.md)). The card's head leads with its icon in a 28 px frame (`--surface-muted`, `--border`, `--radius-control`). Between such cards 24 px on a page, 16 px in a drawer.
- **Dividers in a segmented box** (BTN-600) — a box of several parts in a row (the account block of the header, the download meter) splits them with 1 px `--border` lines of two heights: a full-height line between different things (the account and its limits, the name and its progress), a short 16 px line, centred, between parts of one thing (one limit window and the next, the bar and its megabytes). A muted 12 px strip of small facts about one thing, without a box, uses the short line alone, 12–14 px high (BTN-613: a session's time, account and id, `.sessions__seg`; a run's commit, number and workflow, `.ci__seg`); what stands apart from them, such as a time or a state, goes to the strip's right end. **How:** the short line is a `linear-gradient` background at the part's left edge (`.hblock__seg.limit + .hblock__seg.limit`, `.cli-copy__bytes`), so it takes no border and no pseudo-element.

## Plaques and labels

- **Plaques** (BTN-94) — a status is a `.chip`, a coloured fill (`running`, a question, a skipped task, a closed one in the stats table); a label is a `.tag`, a neutral outline (a model, the ghost's caption, tracker labels in the card).
- **Count badge** (BTN-1368, `chain.css`) — a number on a tab is a `.count-badge`, an 18 px pill of its own beside the word, never glued to it with ` · `: grey for a plain number (the backlog's count, a running CI's clock), red for a backlog with uncommitted work (the modifier `.count-badge--work`: `--error-bg` with a `--lamp-error` figure), amber or blue for the chats waiting or unread. Its figures are centred as capitals (below). Inside a status plaque ("queued (2)", "uncommitted (49)", BTN-1374) it is a 14 px pill tinted by the plaque's own text colour, a gap after the word (`.chip .count-badge`).
- **Centring by letter height** — text in a plaque or a button is centred by the height of its letters, not by the font's line box. **A repeated trap:** Segoe UI puts lowercase text about 2 px below the middle of its line box (ascent 13 of 16 px at 12 px; SF differs), so a pill with lowercase text ("max", "pro") looks low however `align-items` is set. The box is trimmed with `text-box: trim-both`: to the x-height for lowercase text, to the capital height (`cap`) for capitals and figures.
  - `.tag` takes `text-box: trim-both ex alphabetic` with padding `calc((18px - 1ex) / 2)`; a new plaque with lowercase text does the same, one with capitals or figures (`.count-badge`) trims to `cap`.
  - `.button` takes `text-box: trim-both cap alphabetic` and `padding-block: calc((line-height - 1cap) / 2 + pad)`, so its height is the same in any font. A button with its own `line-height` or padding (`.packages .button`) repeats the rule with its numbers. Firefox has no `text-box` and keeps the plain padding (`@supports` in `controls.css`).
  - Never tune padding by pixels for one font. Check it on a screenshot zoomed 4–5×; at 1× it is not seen.
- **Run label** (BTN-983) — a task's run (provider, model, effort) is `RunLabelTag`, the same on a chain row, in the backlog and in the journal. The provider stands by its short name (`Claude`, `Codex`, `OpenCode`, `Copilot`), the model without everything through its last `/`, then the effort as a scale of five bars a word's space after it (`EffortBarsIcon`, BTN-1369: minimal and low fill one, medium two, high three, xhigh four, max five; `none` and an unknown word draw none; an effort alone stands by itself); `title` holds the run's full text (`taskRunText`) with the effort's word. Only the model takes an ellipsis when space runs out; the provider and the effort stay whole. Wherever an effort is shown next to a model — the chats' cards, the chat's status line, the session's Model fact — it is the same scale (`EffortBars`, `RunLabelTag.tsx`), its word in the tip, never glued with ` · `; its bottom stands on the text's baseline and its tallest bar is a capital's height (`.effort-bars`, `1cap`), so it reads as part of the line. On a chain row the cap of 32 ch is on the run tag, not on the row's right side, so the chips, the session meter, a button and the counters stay one line. The card's labels and role captions use the full property width.

## Bands and notices

- **Band** — `Band` (`tone` `note`, `warn` or `error`) across the card or the page, with its action at the right (the paid-usage warning of the accounts, `OverageWarning`). One short line of state is not a band across the page but a chip in the page's head (BTN-615: "What sessions get" of the project page).
- **The band under the header** (`Banner`) is the same: one line per problem, the text at the left, the action in `banner__actions` at the right edge, kept at the right when it wraps under the text on a narrow screen. An error is the red band itself (`tone="error"`), never a red box inside a band of another colour, nor a title line above it (BTN-601). The name of what it is about stands inside the text, not as a heading: the "copy not downloaded" band (BTN-739) is one line per CLI, "Claude Code: the copy has not been downloaded…", with "Download (≈N MB)" and "Use installed CLI" in `banner__actions`.
- **A chat's band** — what a chat has to say beside its status line is a band over the field (BTN-1363, `ComposerBanner`): a few words, the explanation in a tip, the buttons and ✕ at the right — never text under the field.
- **Folded warnings** — `FoldBand`: one warning band with a count and a short summary, a keyboard toggle, and an action that stays available while folded. Errors stay outside it, above the warnings.
- **Toast** (BTN-845) — `Toast` in `ToastStack` (`web/src/ui/Toast.tsx`, `toast.css`), the page's bottom-right corner. Every toast goes through `ToastList` (several, keyed) or `ToastPresence` (one, `null` when gone), never a bare `Toast` its owner unmounts. A toast that goes by itself takes `timer` (`useToastTimer`): a 3 px `--accent` bar at its bottom shrinks over the time left and stands while the pointer is over the toast or the focus inside it, or the tab is hidden. **How:** both lists are a `FoldList` whose slot grows, fades in and slides up 12 px through `appear`, leaves the same way backwards, and lets the rest of the stack close the gap smoothly; the gap between toasts is `--toast-gap`, the room kept for the shadow is `--toast-shade-*` (why — the comment of `.toast-slot` in `toast.css`). With reduced motion only the fade is left and the bar is hidden.
- **Download** (BTN-600) — `CliProgress` (`web/src/layout/CliCopies.tsx`): one line, the kind of download ("Downloading CLI copy") at the left and, right of it, a meter in a box of its own — `--surface`, 1 px `--border`, `--radius-control` — holding the name of what is downloading, a bar and the megabytes (sizes in its JSDoc). The box, not the band, carries the colour, so the band stays one line high. A new download — a CLI, an update — takes the same look, and its status stays "downloading" until every package of it is in place, so the band does not fold and come back between them.

## Folds, loading, windows

- **Disclosure** — a block that opens in the page flow is `Fold` (`ui/Fold.tsx`, `ui/fold.css`); a native `<details>` is refused (guard). It is a button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, the shared `ChevronIcon` turning through `fast`, and a body whose grid rows change from 0fr to 1fr with opacity through `fold`. Opening by its toggle makes the scroll follow the body as it grows until its end shows, never pushing the toggle out at the top, so a group opened at the bottom of a chat or in its middle shows what it opened. Menus, popovers and the chain's card choreography keep their own motion.
  - **How:** pass a function as children for expensive content: it is called only while mounted. Content mounts on opening, stays through the exit and unmounts after the body's transitions finish; reversing a transition cancels its pending removal. Saved and conditional states stay with the caller through `open`/`onOpenChange` or `defaultOpen`. Closing restores focus from hidden content and, if the header was above the scroll area, brings it back into view. The scroll on opening is `revealBelow`; a faded edge of the scroll box is its `scroll-padding`, as in the transcript. `FoldToggle`/`FoldBody` share the mechanism where the header is separate or the body lives in a table cell. A text `preview` retains the clipped first six lines (`--fold-preview` overrides the height for Markdown). `FoldPresence` retains a disappearing notice until its exit ends; `FoldList` does the same for individual keyed notifications without reordering the remaining ones.
- **Loader** (BTN-618) — a first read shows `Loading` (`web/src/ui/Loader.tsx`, `loader.css`), not a line of text: the logo's boat rocking on a running sea with a 13 px muted line of what is read ("Reading the transcript…"), centred in its room. The task card takes it too (BTN-801: the card holds the phase itself, `useLoader` with `Loader fit="fill"`, standing at its full height while it reads and gliding to the content after, `docs/web/project-page.md`), and so does an agent's turn the window waits on (the changelog). Its own ways stay: a wait inside a line (a share's file) keeps its text, a skeleton keeps its shape (Tools), and `/stats` keeps its own.
  - Its room is `fit`: `block` (160 px of its own), `fill` (the rest of a flex column: a window, so its foot stays at the bottom), `page` (the screen under the header), `menu` (a field's list while its values are read: a 48 px boat, a 12 px line, BTN-1257).
  - **How:** its life is the page's, not the data's (`loaderPhase.ts`, the times in `LOADER_MS`): an entrance (it fades in, the boat rises onto the sea, the sea spreads), a shortest stay, the wait, an exit (the same backwards), and only then the content, so a fast answer still gets the whole motion and never a one-frame flash; something already there at the first render shows no loader at all. It owns its animation, so a container's fade for its children never restarts on it (`loader.css`). With reduced motion only the fade is left.
- **Modal** (BTN-114) — the page behind every modal `<dialog>` — the task card, the session window, the transcript and the chat, the media viewer — is dimmed and blurred alike, by `--scrim` and `--scrim-blur`; only the media viewer keeps its own, darker dimming. A new modal takes both tokens. A dialog is a card: title, content, the primary button at the bottom right.
- **Wizard** (BTN-346) — `WizardFrame` and `WizardFoot` (`web/src/pages/WizardFrame.tsx`): a centred 760 px column, its head, the steps as dots on a line (done filled with a check and a summary under the label, the current a ring, the rest empty, each a link), the step in one card, and its foot — "← Back" a quiet link, the primary "Next: <step> →" at the right, "Skip for now" on an optional step; Enter is next. `/setup` and `/projects/add` use it; a new wizard takes it rather than a list of steps of its own. A dot is `StepDot`, shared with the project wizard (BTN-344), whose steps are settings cards all shown at once, each led by a dot of its check: empty, a ring for the first unfilled, green with a check when passed, red when failed with the reason under the field. A choice inside it is a card that rises on hover, the picked one with the accent's edge and a check in its corner (`ChosenMark`).

## Code and commands

- **Command** (BTN-432) — a command the page asks a person to run is a `CommandBlock`, a code block of its own, never mono text inline in a band: `--bg` with a border, mono 13 px, a long line wrapped inside, with a copy icon in its top right corner that turns into a check for 2 s; without the clipboard (plain HTTP from another host) only selectable text. The folder trust of the project wizard and the marketplace offer of Tools take it.
- **Code block** (BTN-1677) — every fenced block of rendered markdown — chats and transcripts (`SessionMarkdown`), a task's description and comments (`CardText`), the update window — is the `pre` of `react-markdown` through `CodeBlock` (`web/src/ui/CodeBlock.tsx`, `codeBlock.css`), with a copy icon in its top right corner, outside the text so it stays put, and the text's right padding keeping the first line clear of it. The description's editor keeps its own blocks.
  - Where the pointer hovers (`hover: hover`) the icon fades in through `fast` while the block is hovered or the icon has the keyboard's focus, and out after; on a touch screen it always shows.
  - A press copies the block's text without its last line break and turns the icon into a check for 10 s (`COPIED_MS`), shown all that time without hovering; a press on the check copies again and starts the 10 s anew. No clipboard — no icon, as `CommandBlock`.

## Buttons and menus

- **Buttons** — `.button`; `.button--primary`, one per card or dialog: the thing to do next; `.button--small`, a button inside a list row (Done on a chain row), so it does not make the row taller — it is declared after `.button` in `controls.css`, which it would lose to otherwise; `.button--danger`. A greyed button that has a reason is `aria-disabled`, not `disabled`, so the reason is reachable from the keyboard. Its label is centred by letter height (above).
- **Row action** — a row's secondary action that repeats on every row ("Open" on `/log`) is `.row-action` (`controls.css`): a 22 px icon button that shows only while its `.row-action-host` row is hovered or focused, always on a touch screen, with the accent only on itself. "To the backlog" on a chain row and "To Todo" on a backlog row take no room at all until their row is hovered and slide out (BTN-1162, [`docs/web/project-page/chain-and-backlog.md`](../web/project-page/chain-and-backlog.md), "Into the chain and back").
- **⋯ menu** — a rare or destructive action goes into a ⋯ menu, not next to the main one: `MoreMenu` (`web/src/ui/MoreMenu.tsx`, the account card's "Remove"); a menu of its own builds on `usePopoverMenu`. An item that cannot be done is greyed with why under it; an item that cannot be undone (Disconnect a machine, Archive or Delete a chat) is red, `danger`. A card's foot has the framed ⋯; a list row or a window's head the quiet one (`MoreMenu quiet`, BTN-1775), so a list carries no column of outlined buttons. A row that is a button holds its ⋯ as a sibling over its corner, not inside.
- **Close** (BTN-1435) — a button that closes or removes something holds `CloseIcon` (`web/src/ui/icons.tsx`), never the `×` glyph, which sits off its line box's middle (guard). The button centres it with grid or flex, sized as the other icons of its head (16 px in a panel's or the card's 32 px button).
- **Targets** are at least 24 px (WCAG 2.5.8), and in the header 44 px on a narrow screen (760 px and below, "Narrow" in [`docs/design.md`](../design.md)); grown with padding or an invisible `::after`, never with the text.

## Tips

An explanation or a quiet detail is a tip, not a line of text. Every tip is built on `useTip` (`web/src/ui/useTip.ts`); what a field's "?" holds is in [`fields.md`](fields.md), how a tip of several facts reads is "Facts are never glued" in [`docs/design.md`](../design.md).

- **Tip** — `Tipped` (`web/src/ui/Tipped.tsx`) opens once the pointer has rested on it `OPEN_DELAY_MS` (700 ms), so a pass of the mouse over a row opens nothing, and at once on focus or a press; a tip open or closed within `WARM_MS` (300 ms) makes the next pointed one open at once — that state is the page's, shared by every tip (EKR-28, the pattern of Windows, macOS and VS Code). It closes `CLOSE_DELAY_MS` after the pointer leaves the anchor.
- **No native `title`** in or around a tip's anchor: it is too slow and goes unnoticed, and inside our tip's anchor or around it both would open at once, the system's over ours (EKR-146). What the `title` said goes into the tip as a line of its own (a chain row's priority under its cache ring); `npm run shot` lists any such place as `double`.
- **A tip of text takes no pointer** (`pointer-events: none` on `.tip`, `.help__tip`, `.panel__split`, BTN-208) and its text cannot be selected: its popover sits inside the anchor in the DOM, so a pointer over it would keep the anchor hovered and the tip open. Only a tip with buttons takes the pointer — the related tasks of `Links` (`.links__tip`).
- **The "?" of a setting** (`HelpTip`) is a toggletip: only a press opens and closes it, Escape and a press outside close it, pointing and focus do nothing.
- **Action with help** — `ButtonWithTip` (`web/src/ui/ButtonWithTip.tsx`) joins an action button to a separate `?` button at its right edge. The main action keeps its handler; the help opens as `HelpTip` does. Both buttons remain in the Tab order.
- **Closing** — every tip closes on a scroll of the page or of a box outside it and on a window resize; a scroll inside the tip keeps it open (how: `useTip`'s JSDoc).
