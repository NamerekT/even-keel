# Chain: the exceptions to invariant 1

Invariant 1: the dispatcher does not touch a session that has stopped. Below is the closed list of exceptions to it, then the two writes to a session without a stop and the keep-warm ping, which only reads it. A new way to touch a session is a new entry here. Words like mid-turn, busy, warm and handoff are those of [`docs/chain.md`](../chain.md#words-of-the-area).

**Where a touch runs.** The machine the session runs on makes every touch of a session that already runs — exceptions 1–3, 6, 8–10 and 13, the paid-usage stop and the temporary-error retry — as the link's commands `task.<move>` the hub sends (`MachineMoves`, BTN-1221, `docs/machines/seam.md`, "Moves"). The hub decides when. A machine of its own whose link is down decides itself, by the same code over the hub's last move call, for the limit, the temporary-error retry, the checkpoint, a move a restart cut short and the paid-usage stop of the tasks the hub gave it (BTN-1226); every other touch waits for the hub.

**A stop** in every exception goes through the session's agent (`claude stop`, never `rm`): the transcript stays and `--resume` by hand is possible.

## The list

1. **Limit** on the session of the current task of an unpaused chain.
   - **When it goes on.** A minute after the limit resets, or at once when the account is switched. An agent may round the reset down to the minute; a reset already over a minute gone at the refusal waits 10 minutes from it (EKR-34, `docs/sessions.md`).
   - **Warm or not.** The dispatcher resumes the session with `--resume` while its cache is warm: the same account, within the session's warm window since its last response — 55 minutes for Claude on a subscription, 25 for Codex and Copilot (`docs/sessions/cache.md`, "Every agent's cache"). A keep-warm ping that read the cache counts as the last response (`HandoffSession.warmedAt`, `warmSince`). Otherwise it stops the session and starts a handoff in its working folder (`src/chain/relay.ts`). Every other exception that says "warm" means this rule.
   - **What is a limit.** The `rate_limit` error, and the CLI's soft warning (`usageLimitNote: "wrap_up"`) after which the session wraps up its turn by itself.
   - **Where the reset time comes from.** The limit's text. The warning has none and comes before the window shows 100%, so its reset comes from the account's limits polled after the limit (`limitResetAfter`, `src/limits/model.ts`): the exhausted window's reset, else the end of the five-hour window the limit fell into, or the start of a newer one. Until such limits arrive the reset is unknown (`resetsAt: null`, the page says only an account switch helps), and every check asks again.
   - **An early reset** (BTN-530). A poll taken after the refusal that shows the account open again ends the wait as the reset would, a minute after the poll (`limitReopenedAt`; `Handovers.reopenEarly`). ChatGPT's `wham/usage` says so itself (`rate_limit.allowed: true`, `limit_reached: false`, `RateLimits.open`). A Claude `/usage` reading counts as open when no window is at 100% — the five hours, the week, the month and the own week of the task's current model — and no window resetting within an hour of the named reset is still at 90% or more (`STILL_SPENT_PERCENT`). A window started over resets later; an early reset of the week empties the meter and keeps its end; a Fable reset takes Fable's share off the week, so a task held on Fable's week and moved to another model goes on at the next poll. A poll less than 10 minutes before the named reset is no early reset (`REOPEN_MARGIN_MS`): `/usage` drops the window minutes before the CLI lets requests through. A statusline, a session's `rate_limit_event`, Copilot, keys and spending caps keep their own paths.
   - **A limit nothing tells.** An account with no known window whose poll said there is nothing to read (`noKnownLimit`: OpenCode Zen, Copilot signed in on the machine, BTN-342) is tried again instead (`LimitRetries`, `src/limits/retry.ts`): 15 minutes after the hit, doubling up to 4 hours while each retry hits the limit again, back to 15 minutes once the account worked longer than its last wait. In memory: a restart starts over.
   - **An API key** (`docs/accounts.md`) has no windows to wait out: its `rate_limit` stops the session like any other error. Two refusals hold it instead. Spent credit (`billing_error`, `apiError.kind: credit`, BTN-165) waits until a key check finds credit again (`limitRecovery` in `src/limits/recovery.ts` asks `AccountWatcher.creditBackAfter`). A spending cap (`apiError.kind: spend`, BTN-382) takes its UTC reset from the refusal, and the five-minute key checks go on: a 2xx allows an earlier continuation. A key's warm window is its own (`HandoffSession.warmMs`): under 55 minutes with a 1-hour cache in the transcript, else under 4 minutes, since a key writes the 5-minute cache unless `cacheTtl` is `1h`. A switch of the account hands over at once.
   - **The folder and the branch** of the handoff come from git (the worktree root from the last `cwd`), not from the transcript, whose `gitBranch` is the launch folder's. A repository nested in that folder that is no worktree of the same repository (a clone the session made under `.temp/`) is skipped for the one around it (`locateWorktree`): the CLI would refuse the clone as an untrusted folder.
   - **Paid usage** (BTN-577, `OverageStops.guard`). Fresh evidence of paid subscription usage stops the chain's current live session, mid-turn too, through its `Agent.stop` — even while the chain is paused or waits for "Stop after the turn". How it is detected: [limits](../sessions/limits.md#stopping-paid-subscription-usage-btn-577). An in-flight move is recovered first. `LimitHold.overage` keeps the whole `LimitedSession` and a mark of the stop that went through, so recovery works after the session is gone and after a restart mid-handoff. Then it waits with no second stop and no relaunch; an unknown reset stays unknown and does not enter the retry ladder. A new account whose included window is still spent (a shared ChatGPT pool too) keeps the wait. Once usable, the usual resume or handoff keeps the worktree, branch and context. A pause still holds the continuation, unattended mode never skips this wait, and the hold ends early by the early-reset rule above (EKR-125). Real paid usage is [unverified](../unverified/accounts.md#paid-subscription-usage-stops).
   - **A temporary refusal** of the model or the service (`apiError.kind: transient`, BTN-498). `Handovers.followTransient` retries the current session of an unpaused chain with `LimitRetries` per session: 15 minutes, doubling after each further refusal up to four hours, back to the start after a good answer or a person's continuation. It resumes the same session with a short continuation prompt, warm cache or not, and never makes a handoff. No retry mid-turn, during a move, without a ready account or while launches are blocked. A chat answer or "New session" cancels a scheduled retry, and the last continuation time keeps an old refusal in the transcript from scheduling it again. The limit hold carries the next try on the page and through a restart; the ladder is in memory. It does not depend on usage windows or credit. Not seen through a real outage yet ([unverified](../unverified/sessions.md#temporary-model-errors-btn-498)).
   - **The log** records each new limit once: the task, the account folder, the reset (or `reset unknown`) and the plan. A learned or earlier reset logs the new plan. A continuation logs the resume or handoff, the new session id (and the previous one for a handoff) and its cause: `limit`, `credit` or `transient` (BTN-862). A failed try uses the chain's pause log.
2. **"New session"** (`restart`): the user replaces the current task's session when it is not mid-turn — a handoff, `restart` in the launch log, naming the previous session.
   - A working turn silent for five minutes can be replaced too, after a confirmation tied to that session and its last write; new output voids the confirmation, also while the replacement is queued. After thirty quiet minutes the turn counts as hung and needs no confirmation. It is still the user's replacement, never an automatic stop. "Skip" stays refused mid-turn.
   - A chat reply to a session whose cache went cold may take the same way at the user's choice (BTN-757, `docs/sessions/chat.md`, "A reply to a cold session"): `Dispatcher.renew` refuses what `answerable` refuses (exception 6) and a loose session, then makes a handoff with the message at the end of the prompt; the log says `restart` with cause `message`.
3. **Checkpoint** the session asked for itself: the current task's session of an unpaused chain finished a turn whose last line is `CHECKPOINT: <path>`, or a bare `CHECKPOINT:` after a note given to the tasks server's `handoff_write` (BTN-1046, `docs/sessions/checkpoint.md`; the note's text then goes into the prompt in place of the path).
   - The `agent-bridge@even-keel` plugin's hook asks for it when the context has grown; its `Stop` hook sends back a session that wrote the note but ended on a report without the mark.
   - The dispatcher makes a handoff with the note's path from the worktree root in the prompt, `checkpoint` in the launch log.
   - A tool call after the mark, a new response without it or a message to the session cancels it.
   - Handoffs repeat as often as sessions ask; the page shows their count, and after the third one the hook asks only from 300k (`docs/sessions/checkpoint.md`, "Checkpoint threshold").
   - A session on a limit goes by exception 1 (its prompt points at the note too); while the limit is spent or new sessions are not started, the mark waits.
4. **Task in Done**: once its session is not busy (a turn that ended on a background command counts as over), the dispatcher stops it and removes the worktree ([`cleanup.md`](cleanup.md)).
   - The same stop follows every other way a task reaches Done: a `NO CHANGES:` mark (EKR-10) and "Accept as answer" (BTN-885) — the press only comments and moves the task, never writes to the session — both in [`cleanup.md`](cleanup.md), "Nothing to merge" and "Accept as answer"; a fix session's mark once main is green at the red commit or past it (EKR-88, BTN-1328, [`main-ci.md`](main-ci.md), "Nothing to fix" and "Main fixed outside the chain"). A fix session without the mark is only let go then, never stopped.
   - Nothing is stopped or removed before the monitor's first read of the sessions since the service's start (BTN-646, [`cleanup.md`](cleanup.md), "The first read of the sessions").
   - Another session in the task's folder found mid-turn is stopped once its turn ends (BTN-616, [`cleanup.md`](cleanup.md), "A session left mid-turn").
   - After the merge, sessions make no more than a couple of checks in the same turn and finish with a report (transcripts, 2026-09-29).
5. **A slot is switched** to another task's branch, only when the pool has no free slot: first the live sessions of released tasks in the slot are stopped, else an answer through Remote Control would land in another task's branch ([`worktrees.md`](worktrees.md), "Slot" mode).
   - Such a session mid-turn: the slot is not switched and the chain pauses with its task; unattended, it waits for the slot as when none is fit.
   - **A project without git** (BTN-962): its folder is every task's slot. Before a task's session starts there, the live sessions the launch log gives other tasks in it are stopped the same way (`vacateSlot` with `shared`, from `wirePlaces.place`), so one session works there at a time even after a skip or a postponement. One mid-turn holds the launch with `folder.busy` in any mode — the chain waits for its turn, retried every 30 s, without a pause; a refused stop pauses with `folder.stopFailed`. A session started there by hand is not the chain's and is left alone.
   - A task released so, whose session was stopped, comes back to a fresh session, not a handoff.
6. **The user's answer** (BTN-101) from the chat window (`POST /api/session/answer`, `docs/web.md`) or from a tracker comment after the question Even Keel posted there (`TrackerQuestions`, `docs/trackers.md`).
   - A pending `AskUserQuestion` cannot be closed from outside, so the dispatcher stops the session and resumes it with the answer (`Dispatcher.answer` → `deliver`, the path of exception 1; `answer` in the launch log; `resumedAt` reset). The stop loses the session's last message, which the CLI had not written yet, so the prompt repeats the question (`replyPrompt`, `docs/sessions.md`).
   - Files are saved first into `.temp/inbox/` of the task's folder (`src/sessions/inbox.ts`).
   - `Dispatcher.answerable` refuses unless all of these hold:
     - the task is its chain's current one (`chain.notCurrent`) and is not being launched (`chain.launching`) — or its chain is stopped and its session is loose: `Handovers.deliverLoose` resumes it and logs `answer`, a failure goes only to the log;
     - the session is the one the user answered, and it waits on the same open question (`ask` id, or none for a question in plain text) — else `answer.moved` (the user answered through Remote Control, say);
     - it is not mid-turn (`answer.busy`; a plain message to a working session is the second write below);
     - launches are allowed and the limit is not spent (`chain.limitSpent`).
   - The answer to a permission dialog takes another path: the first write below.
7. **A red main** with `ci.fixTask` off (BTN-115): the current task's session that is mid-turn in the lane that runs the fix (`MainFix.fixer`, BTN-995: with several lanes, only one) is stopped so that it does not work on a broken `main`, and continued with a message once the fix has landed. A session that waits for an answer is not stopped: a new message would wipe its open question. In detail — [`main-ci.md`](main-ci.md), "The fix session".
8. **Feedback on a pull request** in PR mode (BTN-205): a review, a failed run or a conflict the poll found goes to the task's session only while it is not mid-turn, by `--resume` while warm, else by a handoff that carries it.
   - A mark on a branch with nothing beyond main and uncommitted work, and a mark whose push the forge refused as behind (BTN-716), are told to the session by `--resume`; with a clean worktree the task goes to Done and exception 4 stops its sessions (BTN-222).
   - A pull request closed unmerged stops the task's idle sessions at its cleanup, as a Done does.
   - In detail — [`pr-mode.md`](pr-mode.md).
9. **A task back from an inactive status** (BTN-245): the current task left the chain for an inactive status (Backlog and the like) while its session was not busy — the unattended case: the session asks in a tracker comment, moves the task to Backlog and ends its turn in plain text.
   - The dispatcher shelves the task and its session (`shelve` in `src/chain/shelved.ts`). A release by a blocker or "Skip" is shelved with its own reason (exception 10); "Stop" is not shelved.
   - When the task is active again and the chain takes it, the old session is stopped and a handoff starts (`returned` → `Handovers.handOverCurrent`; `relay` in the launch log and the snapshot). Its prompt says the task came back from the user after a question and the answer is in the description or the comments after it — to be read in spite of the tracker skill's rule against the activity feed. Such a comment is not one of Even Keel's tracker answers (exception 6 reads only answers to questions Even Keel posted), and the old cache is cold by then.
   - The old session is taken on instead, with no stop, when it is busy by then, or its feed has a prompt or an answer after the task left (someone talked to it through Remote Control or the chat of a stopped chain).
   - A busy session keeps its task in the chain (`enters`): a task leaves only once its session's turn has ended.
   - The handoff waits for the account's limit and the project's packages like a launch, and not for the CI of main, like a handoff after a limit.
   - `shelved` goes with `released`: taking the task again or cleaning it up forgets it.
10. **A task back after a blocker or "Skip"** (BTN-547): a release with `postpone` or `skip` shelves the session with the time, the reason and the blockers, unless it waits on its own `AskUserQuestion`. The release itself only stops waiting for the session.
    - When the task can run again and the chain takes it, the safeguards of exception 9 apply: no turn under way, no open question, no prompt or answer since the release; otherwise the old session is taken on.
    - An idle session is stopped and replaced by a handoff on the same branch and worktree; `relay` with cause `unblocked` or `unskipped` in the launch log and the snapshot.
    - An unblocked prompt names the closed blockers; an unskipped one says "Skip" was undone, assuming no question in Backlog.
    - Limits and packages hold the handoff as in exception 9; a move a restart cut keeps its reason and blockers.
11. **A project chat goes to the archive** (BTN-1230, `docs/sessions/project-chats.md`): its session is stopped through its agent (`MachineChats.stop` → `stopIn`), in any state, mid-turn and waiting on a question too.
    - When: a day after the chat's last message or session action, or when the user hides it (Archive of the chat's ⋯ menu, BTN-1775).
    - **An idle chat session** is stopped the same way with the chat staying active (BTN-1653, `docs/sessions/project-chats.md`, "An idle session"): when its cache went cold (`cold`), and when a chat session past the `chatSessions` ceiling comes up on its machine — then the machine's longest idle chats. Never one `starting`, `working` or `waiting`, never a chain session.
    - A chat has no task. Its session's only other touch is the user's own message from its window (`send` in `src/service/machine-chats.ts`), tried in this order:
      1. a call the session waits to have approved takes the message as its decision (BTN-1769, the first write below);
      2. a session that steers takes it into its live CLI (`Agent.reply`);
      3. a live `--bg` session takes it through its inbox socket, mid-turn too (BTN-1345);
      4. mid-turn, a session whose hooks listen for mail gets it in its mailbox (the second write below); any other mid-turn session refuses it with `chats.busy`;
      5. not mid-turn: a stop and `--resume` with the message.
12. **A project chat replaced by a new session** (BTN-1232): the old chat's session is stopped the same way, mid-turn too, before anything else changes (a failed stop refuses the whole step), and the chat goes to the archive with its successor (`ProjectChats.renew`). "Continue in new session…" of its ⋯ menu (BTN-1776, `ProjectChats.continueEarly`) is the same stop ahead of a cold cache or a limit, by the user's choice; the new chat's session waits for the user's first message.
13. **A landing returned to the session** (BTN-1581, `Landings`, `src/chain/landings.ts`): the session merged into the main checkout and its turn ended, and the push of `main` met a conflict with the remote's `main` or a red `commands.check` on the merge with it; `main` was rolled back ([`worktrees.md`](worktrees.md), "Landing").
    - The message — the conflicting files or the red command with its output, and "merge `<remote>/<main>` into your branch, resolve, check, merge again" — goes to the session only while it is not mid-turn: `--resume` while warm, else a handoff that carries it (reason `landing`).
    - It waits in `ChainState.landings` while the account's limit or a launch block holds, so a restart delivers it.
    - A fix or carry session gets the same.
    - Three rounds at most: the fourth is a pause with `landing.exhausted`, and nothing more goes to the session.
    - A remote out of reach or a refusal sends the session nothing.
14. **A task that became a parent** (BTN-1652): a released task that the tracker's read shows open with subtasks — its session split it, or someone else did. It waits for them hours or days, and its window on the page only reads the transcript (BTN-1041).
    - Once its session is not busy (a turn that ended on a background command counts as over) and waits on no question or permission of its own (`asked`), the dispatcher stops it (`MachinePort.stop`; the parent's window reads the transcript from disk) and logs `chain: <task> became a parent, its session <id> is stopped` (`Cleanups.stopParents`, `src/chain/cleanup.ts`).
    - Only the session of the task's newest launch log entry is the chain's (`lastSession`): one started in the folder by hand is left alone. Each session is stopped once (kept in memory).
    - The worktree and the branch stay: the parent is cleaned up at its Done, by exception 4.
    - No threshold of the context: a stopped session goes on by `--resume` with its context, and past the warm cache a live process saves nothing.

**A session the CLI shows `failed` or `stopped`** is stopped as well in every exception while its process lives (`pid` from a live `sessions/<pid>.json`): a session that failed at start (an API 400) keeps running, and `claude stop` from a terminal marks the job `stopped` without reaching the service's daemon (`isGone` in `src/claude/cli.ts`, `stopTaskSessions`, `slotOccupants`). The other way round, a job the CLI still shows `blocked` after its daemon dropped it and exited is gone and needs no stop (`abandoned`, `docs/sessions.md`).

**Not a touch.** The dispatcher may stop waiting for a session that is not busy without touching it: when the current task got an open blocker from the same chain (the session postponed it, `postpone` in the log) or the user pressed "Skip" (`skip`). A skipped task is not taken again until the user returns it or it leaves the chain in the tracker (closed, in Backlog); on its return exception 10 decides between a handoff and the old session. "Skip" is refused for any task whose session is mid-turn, also one released by "Stop": a restarted chain would launch the next task beside the live session instead of taking it again. Unattended mode's skips are the same ([`unattended.md`](unattended.md)).

## Writes without a stop

Two writes reach a session without stopping it. Both carry only the user's own act.

### The answer to a permission dialog (BTN-269)

A Claude session waiting on a permission dialog held by the `agent-bridge` plugin's hook (`docs/sessions/chat.md`, "Permission dialogs") has the call as its question. An answer from the page or a tracker comment goes through `Dispatcher.answer` as any answer, but `resumeWith` hands the decision to the hook instead of stopping and resuming: the session goes on in the same turn with the same id. A project chat's call takes the user's message as its decision the same way (BTN-1769). Nothing else decides such a call; in unattended mode, with nobody at the page, the call is refused at once, as for an SDK session (BTN-303).

### A message to a working session (BTN-181)

A chat message in plain text to the current task's session while it is mid-turn.

- **Admitted** when the session steers or its hooks have asked for mail since the service started (`Mailbox.listens`): `Dispatcher.answerable` then gives `midTurn`, skipping the launch and limit checks since nothing is launched. Otherwise it is refused with `answer.busy`.
- **How it goes in**, by the first that applies:
  - a session that steers (`AgentEntry.steers`, an SDK session, BTN-291) takes it into its live CLI by `Agent.reply` after the running tool call, in the same turn;
  - a Claude `--bg` session whose inbox socket Even Keel knows (BTN-1322, `docs/sessions/chat.md`, "The inbox socket") gets it written into the socket at once, a turn on a background command too; the hooks are the fallback when the write fails;
  - otherwise the message goes into the `mailbox` table, and a hook of `agent-bridge@even-keel` takes it on the session's next tool call or stop and hands it over without ending the turn (`docs/sessions/chat.md`, "Chat mailbox").
- **Between turns.** The same socket write carries the user's own message or plain answer to such a session that stands between turns: exception 6's `resumeWith` tries it first. A session waiting on an `AskUserQuestion` or a permission dialog, or marked hung, is stopped and resumed as before. A session waiting on a question gets the chat as an answer (exception 6), never mail.
- **Mail the hooks missed** — the turn ended, or the session asked, before the next tool call — is settled once the session is no longer mid-turn and 10 s have passed since the post: a turn ended without a question gets it by `--resume` (`Dispatcher.answer`, event `resume`); a session that asked meanwhile, or is gone, gets nothing — the message goes back to the chat window, and the user sends it as the answer or dismisses it.
- **OpenCode** (BTN-1323): its plugin posts the message as the user's own line; a turn that ended before the model answered that line is resumed once with the same text, on the same terms (`answerPosted` in `settleMail`).
- **A task over for the chain** (BTN-558) gets no message: done in the tracker's last read or after its cleanup, or its branch merged into `main` before the tracker knows (`Dispatcher.closed`, the `merged` check of the CI of main). What it asks would land uncommitted in a closed task's worktree, which no merge closes again. The chat refuses such a send with `answer.closed`, an answer to a question too. A message posted before the merge goes neither to the hook (`mailFor` returns nothing) nor by `--resume`; it comes back to the chat with the reason `closed`, without "As the answer": what it asks is a new task.

## A keep-warm ping (BTN-437, `src/sessions/keepwarm.ts`)

The touch that neither stops nor writes: a fork of the session reads its cache, and the session itself is only read. What the fork is, its flags, the page, the timing and the memory over a restart are in `docs/sessions/cache.md`, "Keep-warm"; here is whom it pings and what keeps it safe.

- **The setting.** "Keep the cache of a waiting session warm, hours" (`keepWarm`, Even Keel's own, 2 by default, 0 — off). A check every 5 s (`KEEP_WARM_TICK_MS`) reads only facts in memory.
- **Whom** (`warmTasks`), only on an agent with `Agent.warm` (Claude Code, Codex):
  - the current task's session of each unpaused, unhalted chain not waiting on a limit — unless the task is starting or being moved (`starting`), or its limit is not handled yet or plans a handoff. A temporary error scheduled for a resume stays eligible, and so does the new session of a handoff after a limit (`limit.acted: 'relay'`, BTN-1507);
  - the session a red main stopped mid-turn (`chain.interrupted`, per lane) while its chain waits for the fix, unless the task was skipped or released since (BTN-1616): the chain resumes it once the fix is judged. A red fix that gives it back (`giveBack`) makes it the paused chain's current task, no longer pinged. Its page view is finished, so the check finds it by its task and session (`viewOf`);
  - the live session of each task whose pull request is open in such a chain's project.
- **When not** (`warmStep`, from facts read anew each check: the transcript's cache window, mid-turn, an open call, a turn ended on a background job, a resume or handoff hold, a limit lamp): while a model request of the turn is in flight, while the session is resumed or handed over, on a limit, or when the cache is already cold.
  - A turn ended on a background command or agent is busy for the chain but has no request in flight, so it is pinged like a waiting one (BTN-1507).
  - A turn waiting on a tool call's result — a long build or test run — is pinged like a waiting one where its agent's fork reads such a session (`Agent.warmsOnTool`: Claude Code, `--bg` and SDK, EKR-142). Codex is not: its turns wait for their end.
  - A session stopped for the fix of main may have a transcript ending on an open call (`WarmFacts.stopped`): it is pinged only where its agent's fork reads such a session or its turn had ended, so a Codex one is left to cool.
  - A session waiting on an `AskUserQuestion` is pinged like any other (its fork sends the session's own prefix, BTN-705).
- **How early.** 5 minutes before an hour or a half-hour cache (Codex's) ends, 90 s before a five-minute one; as the update process is about to stop the service, at once for every cache that would go cold within 4 minutes (`KeepWarm.ahead`, BTN-1527).
- **Ceilings.**
  - `keepWarm` hours since the session's last answer;
  - the break-even (`BREAK_EVEN`): 20 pings of an hour cache, 12 of a half-hour or five-minute one, counted at a cache read of 0.1× the input price (cheaper on some models — why the ceiling stays is in the constant's comment);
  - a ping that read under 90% of its prompt from the cache turns pinging off for that session until a restart, and so do three failures in a row for a non-transient reason (`MAX_FAILURES`; an answered ping resets the count). A fork that ended on a tool call but printed its usage counts by that usage. An overload refusal skips that ping only.
- **For the chain** a ping that read the cache is the session's last warmth (exception 1): an answer, PR feedback or the end of a main fix an hour after the last answer still goes by `--resume` if pings kept the cache since.
- **What is seen of it.** No transcript; the page learns of the pings from the machine (`KeptWarm`, EKR-143), Tally from `warm_pings` (BTN-1413), and the service log has a `keepwarm:` line per ping. The series is in memory and taken again after a restart from the hub's `warm_pings` (`KeepWarm.restore`).

## A service restart in the middle of a move (BTN-164)

While the dispatcher resumes or replaces the session of a running task, `CurrentTask` holds `starting` and the kind of move (`move`: `answer`, `resume`, `relay`, `restart`, `message`, `checkpoint`, `review`, `returned`, `unblocked`, `unskipped` — the `Move` type of `src/chain/model.ts`), and for an answer or a new session with the user's message its text and the question it answers (`reply`, `ask`). After a `message` move the new session keeps the user's message in `message` for the chat's handoff view until the next move (BTN-1281, `docs/sessions/chat.md`). A landing's message is no move: it waits in its own record (exception 13).

Loading the state keeps these marks for a task with a session; a cut first launch is dropped and pauses the chain with `chain.interrupted`. The chain's next step sees the move through (`Handovers.recover`) before the limit and the checkpoint:

- no session of the task read yet — it waits;
- the task already has another session — the chain takes it and logs the move;
- the old session works, replied after the move began or waits on another question — the move went through (an answer or a resume is logged);
- otherwise the move is made again: the answer or the feedback delivered once more (`review` keeps its text in `reply`), "New session" and the handoff of a returned task repeated with the same reason and blockers, the limit step redone, a checkpoint left to its mark, which is still the last line.

Within one process the step never sees these marks: moves and steps run one after another. The marks are transient, like `answering`, so they did not change `SCHEMA_VERSION`.

## What a handoff leaves

- **A snapshot.** Every handoff (a limit, "New session", a checkpoint, a returned, unblocked or unskipped task) writes `<state>/handoffs/<new session>.json` (`src/chain/snapshots.ts`): the whole prompt, the note's path and its text as read just before the launch, the reason in the launch log's words and the previous session. The note file is overwritten by the next handoff and goes with the worktree; its text read for the snapshot is also kept in `handoff_notes` as the previous session's note, beside the notes given to `handoff_write` (BTN-1046). Tally loads the snapshots into `handoffs` (`docs/tally.md`). A failed save is a warning in the log; the handoff goes on.
- **Past a session that never answered** (BTN-650: no `lastReplyAt` and no text or tool line — a bad key, no credit, a refusal at the start). `Handovers.handOver` remembers by task the session each handoff started and the answered session it carried on from (`HandoffSession` with its transcript path, in its own account's folder). A handoff from the failed session takes the steps, the last message, the note and the main transcript from the remembered one, and adds a line per failed session after it with its last API error and its transcript (`answered`, `failedOf` in `src/chain/relay.ts`, `PromptTexts.failed`); several failed ones in a row add up. The folder, the branch, the stop and the reason stay the failed session's. The memory does not outlive a restart: then the handoff carries the failed session alone ([unverified](../unverified/sessions.md#a-handoff-past-a-failed-session-btn-650)).
