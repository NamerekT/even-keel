# Sessions: the checkpoint threshold

## Checkpoint threshold (BTN-86)

The `checkpoint` plugin's hook (`plugins/checkpoint/hooks/context-checkpoint.mjs`, the rules in `nudges.mjs`) asks a session for a handoff by context size. The first nudge is at 200k, the next every 50k up to 250k and every 25k past it. The hook reads no transcript, so it works the same for Claude Code and Codex, and for OpenCode through the package's OpenCode plugin ("OpenCode" above): on every tool call it asks the service about its session, `GET /api/sessions/<id>/checkpoint` → `{ project, task, handoffs, first, source, context }`, 404 for a session the chain did not start. `context` is the session's context fill as the service's monitor last read it from the transcript (`SessionMonitor.contextOf`), `null` while it has not; with no answer, a 404 or a `null` the hook is silent, so a session started by hand gets no nudges. The `CHECKPOINT_*` variables still override the steps. Environment variables are not used to pass the answer: the environment of a `claude --bg` does not reach its session while the daemon runs (`docs/accounts.md`).

The plugin's `Stop` hook (`checkpoint-mark.mjs`) sends back a turn that wrote `.temp/handoff.md` among its last three tool calls but ended without the `CHECKPOINT:` line. Whether it did comes from `GET /api/sessions/<id>/note` → `{ note }` (`noteAtEnd` in `src/sessions/note.ts` over the agent's whole `feed`). It recognizes file tools (`Write`, `Edit`, `MultiEdit`) and literal shell write targets: .NET `WriteAllText`/`WriteAllLines`/`AppendAllText`, `Set-Content`/`Add-Content`/`Out-File` with the path first or immediately after a path parameter, and `>`/`>>`. Reads and quoted text mentioning the note do not count. A path built in a variable or a write hidden in another script is not inferred; the session must end with the mark itself.

- `handoffs` (`handoffsOf` in `src/chain/launches.ts`) is the number of the task's sessions started by `checkpoint`, `restart` or `relay`, up to and including this one. From the third on the hook is silent below the hard threshold of 300k: on tasks with many handoffs most of them lost money (CBL-596: 12 handoffs, $17.5 spent, $5.3 saved).
- `first` with `source: "calibrated"` or `"config"` replaces the hook's own first threshold; `"default"` leaves it alone.
- `source: "off"` (BTN-255) — the project's `contextHandoffs` is off, its own or the shared one (`docs/settings.md`): the hook marks no context at any size, the hard threshold of a held task included, and the agent's own compaction keeps the context. The rest of the plugin stays on — the mark of a note the session wrote by itself, the chat's mail, the standards, the check before the merge — and so do the handoffs on a limit, "New session" and the answers: the note stays the way of every move between sessions. The other fields are filled as usual, so the page and an older plugin still see the threshold; a plugin before 0.13.0 does not know `"off"` and keeps nudging.
- A default or calibrated threshold over the first share of the session's context window, 72 % unless set (`stats.checkpointShares.first`; `withinWindow` in `src/stats/checkpoint.ts`; the window is the session's, else `context.window`) comes down to it, rounded down to 1k, with `source: "window"`, which the hook takes like `"calibrated"`: past it the agent's own compaction may come first (below). For a Claude session it is also kept before its compaction, at most the window less 32k less 30k (`COMPACTION_GAP`, `BEFORE_COMPACTION`) — the same point as `late` (BTN-506), so a share above that point no longer lifts it. A threshold pinned by `stats.checkpointTokens` (`"config"`) stays as it is, past the window too, and the shares do not apply.
- `late` and `hard` (BTN-259) are the late and hard shares of the same window, 85 % and 95 % unless set (`stats.checkpointShares.late`, `.hard`), rounded down to 1k; the hook takes the lower of each and its own (the default 250k and 300k or `CHECKPOINT_LATE_TOKENS`/`CHECKPOINT_HARD_TOKENS`), so a small window brings the late nudges and the hold of a task with many handoffs inside it and a large one changes nothing. A share therefore only lowers a step of the hook: raising it lifts the step at most to the hook's own value. For a Claude session (`SessionView.agent`) both are also kept before its compaction, below: at most the window less 32k less 30k for `late` and less 15k for `hard` (`COMPACTION_GAP`, `BEFORE_COMPACTION`), room for a large tool result and the note, so a share above that point no longer lifts them. On a 250k window a Claude session's nudges come at 180k, 188k and 213k, a held task is asked from 203k; on a 200k one `first` and `late` come down to the same 138k — the window less 32k less 30k, under the 144k first share and the 170k late one — so the nudges come every 25k, at 138k, 163k and 188k, and a held task is asked from 153k, the window less 32k less 15k; on a 400k one the late share 340k is cut to 338k and the hard 380k to 353k. Other agents, and a session the service does not see, keep the shares alone: their point is not measured. Without a window, or with a pinned `first`, both are `null` and the hook keeps its own — a pin of 999999 still silences the nudges below it, though `contextHandoffs` is the switch for that.
- Where Claude Code compacts (measured 2026-10-24, the week of BTN-254 with the nudges off): not at a share of the window but about 32k short of it — 56 auto-compactions in 37 chain sessions on a 250k window came at 216–227k (median 218k), hand sessions on a 400k window at a median 369k. Each session went on from its summary to the task's end. So on a 250k window the late share at 212k came ~5k before the compaction and the hard one at 237k never, and on a 200k window the compaction (~167k) came before the late 85 %; hence the cap of a Claude session's `late` and `hard` above (BTN-377). Codex's point is not measured (`docs/unverified.md`).
- The shares (BTN-261) are whole percents, `first < late < hard`, set for Even Keel and per project (`docs/settings.md`); `DEFAULT_SHARES` holds the built-in ones. The handler of `/checkpoint` reads the project's current values on every request, so a change applies to the next tool call of a running session.
- The task label `Break | compaction` pins `first` to 999999, so the shares and the late and hard caps do not apply; `Break | handoff` or no label uses the project's threshold. Conflicting labels use the project threshold. The handler reads the latest task list on each request; `/stats` still shows the project threshold.
- Calibration (`src/stats/checkpoint.ts`, the query `CHECKPOINT` in `src/stats/reader.ts`): `first = base + (note + recovery) / (cache read price × 40 requests)`, rounded to 1k and kept within 150–250k. `base` is the median context of the project's checkpoint successors at their 15th main-thread request, `note` the median spend of a session from its first write of `.temp/handoff.md` to its end, `recovery` the median spend of a successor before its first edit, the price is that of the successors' most frequent model in Tally's `model_prices`. The work left, 40 requests, is a constant, about the lower quartile of what was measured: a measured remainder depends on the threshold itself (a higher one shortens it and pulls the threshold up, the successors' recovery requests inflate it and pull down). The hard threshold and the limit of handoffs are not calibrated.
- It applies once the project has 20 checkpoint successors and 50 sessions that went over 150k; until then, and without statistics, the threshold is 200k. `stats.checkpointTokens` in the config pins it for all projects and switches the calibration off.
- Measured on 2026-10-10: CBL — base 73k, note $0.18, recovery $0.64, 168 successors → 176k; BTN — 79k, $0.18, $0.69, 32 successors → 187k. Why 150k was too early: with a 1 h cache write at $8/M against a read at $0.2/M, rebuilding the context costs as much as re-reading it 40 times, and of 163 CBL handoffs at 150k 93 lost money ($177 spent, $132 saved).
